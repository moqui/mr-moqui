# Attack surface: staff applications (mr-boss and Mantle)

The staff applications: mr-boss and the Mantle (mr-mantle) services it reaches. The framework's administration services that BOSS Admin shows are in [Attack-Surface-Admin.md](Attack-Surface-Admin.md). The token routes mr-boss adds (`/s`, `/u`, `/cal`, `/r`, `/i`) and its rate limits are under [Token routes](Attack-Surface-Routes.md#token-routes). The public applications are in [Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS.md](Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS.md). This is one file of the attack surface map. The index, how to read a row, the defaults and the lock-down advice are in [Attack-Surface.md](Attack-Surface.md).

## Sales

The Sales pipeline dataset (`mantle-sales-pipeline`) is filtered by the registered scope policy MantleSalesScope (`mantle.sales.SalesWorkspaceServices.get#PipelineRowFilter`), computed from the signed-in viewer (another userId refused): the organizations of their sales scope and, without `SALES_TEAM_VIEW`, only the deals assigned now to them or a team of theirs; nothing for a viewer who reads nothing of Sales. Not `get#OrganizationRowFilter` alone, which would show a representative the organization's whole pipeline.

The Sales quote dataset (`mantle-sales-quotes`, read by the quote-expiring alert template) is filtered by the registered scope policy MantleSalesQuoteScope (`mantle.sales.SalesWorkspaceServices.get#QuoteRowFilter`), computed from the signed-in viewer (another userId refused) as the quote lists read: Sales quotes only (never a template, which its document leaves out) and never a placed one, of the organizations of their sales scope and, without `SALES_TEAM_VIEW`, only the quotes assigned to them or a team of theirs; nothing for a viewer who reads nothing of Sales. The stage-age dataset (`mantle-sales-pipeline-stage-age`, the stalled-deal alert's) is the pipeline's document under MantleSalesScope.

Sales' scope (Mantle's SalesAccess.js, behind every `mantle.sales` and Sales `mantle.order` service and the BOSS `ironstone.sales` context services): a user reads Sales only with `SALES_VIEW`, `SALES_TEAM_VIEW` or `SALES_REPORT_VIEW`, in the internal organizations they work for (every internal organization only with `SALES_ALL_ORGANIZATIONS` or as a system administrator; it reads nothing by itself), and inside an organization the leads, opportunities, quotes and orders assigned now to them or a current team of theirs, the organization's all only with `SALES_TEAM_VIEW`; a customer link never widens it, and another organization's or an unassigned record answers as missing (RECORD_UNAVAILABLE) in lists, counts, choices, the palette, exports, Chatter, files and Assist tools alike. An edit needs its own permission (`SALES_OPPORTUNITY_EDIT`, `SALES_ACCOUNT_EDIT`, ...) on a record the user reads and names the revision read; a supplied party, organization or record is checked, never trusted. Accounts (`mantle.sales.AccountServices`, `SalesWorkspaceServices` find#Accounts and the account reads, callable remotely and each checking itself) are parties owned by an organization of the scope or shared (`_NA_`); Commons' `check#ContactAccess` also admits a `SALES_ACCOUNT_EDIT` holder for a current contact of such an account, never a person with a user account and never a delete. `merge#Parties` (PartyMerge.js, in Mantle so every path meets it) admits a contact administrator, a caller with authorization off or the system, and a Sales manager or administrator (`SALES_ACCOUNT_EDIT` with `SALES_TEAM_MANAGE` or `SALES_CONFIGURE`) for two parties of one owner organization of their scope; a cross-organization dependency is refused, never moved. The lifecycle participant (`find#OwnedSalesAssignments`, `transfer#SalesAssignment`) reads and moves another user's deals, open quotes and managed accounts only under `SALES_OWNER_CHANGE` (Admin's identity and administrator groups), to a new owner who works for the record's organization, keeping each deal's team. Assist's Sales tools are the guarded services as the caller: `sales_find_opportunities` and `sales_find_quotes` (read; Assistant and MCP), `sales_preview_quote` (read; Assistant), `sales_create_`, `sales_move_` and `sales_close_opportunity` and `sales_send_quote` (AgclWrite, Assistant only, asked of the user before each). The Comms hooks (`ironstone.sales` SalesComms.js) answer as the user in their Sales scope and refuse a user without Sales: record lookup and the call context give only the deals the user reads; "Create lead" and "Create RFQ" on a message read it as the user may and call `store#LeadIntake` or `create#QuoteRequest` as the user (`SALES_OPPORTUNITY_EDIT` or `SALES_QUOTE_EDIT`), the same message again answering the same record.

Sales' quote lines are priced by `mantle.product.PriceServices.get#SellerProductPrice` (`allow-remote="false"`), the selling organization's authority: the public price rows, the seller's and the customer's own, and the seller's published price lists (a rule's base list included), for a caller without `PRICE_VIEW`; never an agreement's row or list, an average cost or another party's row, and no trace. Only `mantle.order.SalesQuoteServices` calls it, after its own checks of the seller (the caller's sales scope), the customer (an account of it) and the quote (assigned to the caller or their team, or the organization's for `SALES_TEAM_VIEW`).

Sales' approvals (`mantle.order.SalesApprovalServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`): `decide#Quote` and the SalesQuoteApproval handlers need `SALES_APPROVE` in the quote's selling organization (a quote asked for approval is readable by such a holder, never editable through that), the revision the approver read and an approver other than the quote's owner, requester or a priced line's actor unless the rule allows it; a notice decides only the rules it named, and only when the approver may decide each; `request#QuoteApproval` and `propose#Quote` need `SALES_QUOTE_EDIT` on an assigned quote; the defaults and rules need `SALES_CONFIGURE` for the organization and name the revision they replace; a price floor's cost is read on the server and never answered; the pre-approval warnings name a rule, never an amount.

Sales' quote issue (`mantle.order.SalesQuoteIssueServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`): each verb needs `SALES_QUOTE_EDIT` on a quote of the caller's sales scope (another's answers as missing), and `send#Quote` also `SALES_QUOTE_SEND`, the seller's commercial revision on and every approval current; recipients are only the customer and its current contacts; only the quote's owner sends from their own mail account, and the organization's mail goes from the organization's own address, never a person's; `send#Quote` and `respond#Quote` lock the order, and `respond#Quote` admits one response an issue, within its validity and at its revision, and places only through `place#Order`; sends, responses and batches carry operation keys; the ledger's builds read the issue alone and are suppressed once it no longer stands, and `mantle.party.MessageServices.requeue#Delivery` queues again only a failed or waiting delivery; the stored cover, terms and reminder text render locked with an explicit map; the issued PDF is streamed, its location never answered.

Sales' orders (`mantle.order.SalesOrderServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`): each verb needs its permission on an order of the caller's sales scope (another's answers as missing); an order approver (`SALES_ORDER_APPROVE` or `ORDER_APPROVE`) reads the organization's placed and held orders to decide them, never edits, cancels or copies them, and holds or releases only a Manual or Change hold by that type's permission; `approve#SalesOrder` locks the order and records each order rule's decision at the revision read, the order's owner, enterer, placer and a line's price author refused unless the rule allows it, then calls `approve#Order`, whose credit check runs; where the seller's SalesPolicy approval setting is on, `autoApprove#Order` approves no order with a sales part of it and `approve#Order` needs `SALES_ORDER_APPROVE` in the approver's sales scope and those decisions; the only system approval is Sales' own of an accepted net-terms order while that setting is off, a claim Mantle's code sets in the execution context's shared map for that one order and clears when `approve#Order` returns, never a request value; `hold#Order` and `release#OrderHold` answer a user's Manual, Change or Purchase reason on an order of an organization they do not work for as missing, and Sales places and releases only Manual and Change holds; the storeless confirmation goes once for an order's revision and recipient from the organization's own address, the delivery naming the organization so its suppressions apply.

Sales' quote templates (in `mantle.order.SalesQuoteServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`): orders of purpose `OrpSalesQuoteTemplate`, which `mantle.order.OrderServices.update#OrderStatus` keeps Open (so they are never proposed, placed, approved or cancelled), which invoicing refuses and which reservation, credit, store mail, store worklists and the sales summaries leave out; a Sales reader of the organization reads the current ones, and only a template manager (`SALES_QUOTE_EDIT` with `SALES_TEAM_MANAGE` or `SALES_CONFIGURE`) reads the others and changes a draft, at its revision, a published one only through a dated successor; another organization's, or one the caller may not read, answers as missing, and the record rule behind Chatter, files, product connections and the palette (`check#QuoteAccess`) gives the same answer in every app, while Customer Service's orders leave templates out; a template's lines go through the quote line verbs, priced by the seller's authority for no customer; `create#Quote` copies only a current template of the quote's organization, in its currency, and reprices it for the quote's customer as of the quote's date, a fixed line keeping its price and reason with the quote's maker as its price author; `preview#QuoteTemplate` writes nothing and prices only for an account the caller reads. `approve#SalesOrder` records the approver's decision on every approval, and an order approver reads an approved order only when they recorded a decision on it.

Sales' commercial order changes (`mantle.order.OrderChangeServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`, over OrderChanges.js and SalesOrderChanges.js): a price, quantity increase or new line, ship date, address, ship method or carrier change of a sales order is filed under Customer Service's `ORDER_CHANGE_REQUEST`, Sales' `SALES_ORDER_CHANGE` or as the order's editor in Sales, decided under `SALES_APPROVE` (not by its filer unless the organization's policy allows), and applied only under `SALES_ORDER_CHANGE` in the seller's sales scope (Customer Service's order desk is refused `FORBIDDEN`), under the order's lock, at the proposal's and the order's revisions with a current, unexpired approval and an open fiscal period, in one transaction; an active pick, a shipped quantity or a finalized invoice refuses it by name, a packed line's draft invoice is released only through Accounting's `release#ShipmentDraftInvoice` under the applier's own `RECEIVABLE_EDIT`, a new line is priced by `get#SellerProductPrice`, a higher total meets Accounting's credit check, and only its own Change hold is released; another organization's change answers as missing. The applied change's confirmation (`send#OrderChangeConfirmation`, its stored PDF to an address of the customer or its contacts) is sent by Customer Service's applier or by Sales' change operator for an order of their sales scope. The preview shows the customer's credit standing only to `CUSTOMER_CREDIT_VIEW`, `ACCOUNTING_VIEW` or an approver. A change operator reads the organization's placed, processing, approved and held orders and never edits them; `SALES_TEAM_VIEW` alone lists only the commercial kinds; Sales' shipment and invoice reads (`get#SalesShipment`, `check#SalesInvoice`) answer only for an order the user sees in Sales, an invoice only for a receivable reader (`ACCOUNTING_VIEW` or `RECEIVABLE_VIEW`), and a missing or unseen record alike.

Sales' customer agreements (`mantle.party.SalesAgreementServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`): AgrSales and AgrProduct agreements of a selling organization with its customer; a Sales reader of the organization reads them, a change needs `SALES_AGREEMENT_EDIT` and names the agreement's revision, another organization's answers as missing and Purchasing's agreements are not reached; activation needs the customer's signer, acceptance evidence, every pricing item priced in the agreement's currency and parties and no other active agreement with the customer pricing one of its products in its dates. Its negotiated prices change only on the agreement: Products' price editor, bulk changes and imports refuse a customer agreement's row, as they refuse a purchase agreement's. The price resolver applies an agreement's price row or list only while the agreement is active (or has no status) and in its dates, not a template, of the price organization and with the transaction's counterparty, and only for the agreement the caller names; a caller without the price permission reads no party's row whatever agreement or customer it names; `get#SellerProductPrice` reads only the selling organization's current agreements with the quote's customer, and the store's authority none. The agreement's print (`get#SalesAgreementPrint`, AgreementDetail's Print) is rendered from its own records for one who reads it, another organization's or a missing one answering as missing; it carries no cost and no linked order.

Sales' recurring orders (`mantle.order.SalesRecurrenceServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`): a recurring base is an approved copy of a sales order with no payment, marked `OrpRecurringTemplate`, which reservation, shipment, invoicing, a project's order lines, planning, the bulk part status change, the order lists and the sales measures leave out; `SALES_RECURRENCE_MANAGE` keeps the bases of the caller's sales organizations and repeats an approved sales order of them, `SALES_TEAM_VIEW` reads them, and another organization's or a Shop contract's base answers as missing; every change names the base's revision, a retry carries an operation key, turning auto-invoice on needs `RECEIVABLE_EDIT`, and a base's agreement must be an active one of the seller with the customer. The worker (`create#RecurringOrders`, `create#RecurringOrder`, `authenticate="anonymous-all"`, run by the `create_RecurringOrders` job) sends a Shop contract's base to Shop only and orders any other base only when due in its zone, inside its dates and not paused, each in its own transaction, one order for an occurrence under the base's lock, copied with no payment and no purpose (a Sales base's every line, each child line under its own parent, retaxed by its own path, its seller's gateway or its store's; a base made recurring before Sales its product lines) and placed through `place#Order` (so it is approved as any sales order of the seller, never on a copied payment), and fails an occurrence whose agreement no longer applies or that cannot be taxed. `invoice#RecurringOrder` (`authenticate="anonymous-all"`, internal, run only by the `SalesRecurrenceApprovedInvoice` tx-commit rule on an order's approval) reads only the records and bills, through `create#FulfillmentInvoice` and once (each part under its lock), only an approved part of the base's seller of an order the worker made for an occurrence of a base whose auto-invoice is on and that no Shop contract renews. `find#SalesSubscriptions` gives a Sales reader the organization's store contracts (no payment method or consent) and storeless subscriptions; `add#SubscriptionFollowUp` needs `SALES_ACCOUNT_EDIT` and assigns only a person who works for the organization.

Sales' forecasts and targets (`mantle.sales.ForecastServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`): a SalesForecast row a key (organization; a person, a sales team or the organization itself; a period of the organization; a currency), unique (`SALES_FCST_KEY`). A Sales reader sees their own key, a team's manager their teams' and current members', a holder of `SALES_TEAM_VIEW` the organization's; another organization's, another person's or an unseen team's answers as missing. A judgement needs `SALES_FORECAST_EDIT` on one's own key, a team one manages or, with `SALES_TEAM_VIEW`, a team or the organization, never another person's; a target needs `SALES_TEAM_MANAGE` on a key the caller sees, its parent the organization's or a team's forecast of the same organization, period and currency, never a cycle; every write names the revision read; an allocation names a product or category of the organization or a shared one. The measures are computed as read, never stored, from the deals, orders and invoices the reader sees (an invoice only when the reader sees every order it bills), each deal once in a roll-up, never added across currencies, no template or recurring base; a measure past 2,000 rows is refused, never counted short. Orders and invoices with no single representative are shown apart, to a `SALES_TEAM_VIEW` reader only.

Sales' reports (`mantle.sales.ForecastServices` get#PipelineAnalysis and get#WinLoss, `SalesWorkspaceServices.get#SalesReports`, all `allow-remote="false"`): a Sales report reader (`SALES_REPORT_VIEW`) of the organizations of their scope, over the deals they see (their own and their teams', the organization's with `SALES_TEAM_VIEW`); another organization ORGANIZATION_DENIED; amounts never added across currencies; past 2,000 deals refused (TOO_MANY_RECORDS). The catalogue answers the root Sales only. `run#SalesAnalysis` also answers a Sales report reader for an organization of their scope; the cost of shipped and the margin only for a holder of PRODUCT_COST_VIEW who is Accounting's report reader (`ACCOUNTING_VIEW`) or also holds PRICE_VIEW, withheld (null) otherwise, the assets' costs not read; Accounting's Reports/SalesAnalysis is a mount of Sales' screen under Accounting's root authorization.

## Marketing

Marketing's attribution and reports (`mantle.marketing.TrackingServices` record#OrderAttribution, correct#OrderAttribution, run#OrderAttributions; `mantle.marketing.MarketingReportServices`; `SegmentServices.count#Audience`; all `allow-remote="false"`). The decision is made by the order SECA's handler after the placement commits and by the job, as the system, never from a request: the order's local visit and the visitor-key digests that visit's events carried, the visitor-key digest its Shop checkout or ticket payment kept (`OrderHeader.marketingVisitorKeyHash`: a frontend's `submitCheckout` or `startRegistrationPayment` `visitorKey`, of the cookie's form or dropped, kept only as its digest and only where an event would have kept the key, the seller's MarketingPolicy visitorKeys or the customer allowing tracking, never for a customer who withdrew tracking, read again before place#Order; the raw key is never stored), or its customer as a recipient who allows tracking (PcpTracking), consent read when deciding (an event of a recipient who no longer allows tracking is no evidence; a customer who withdrew tracking is attributed by no tracking event); never an automated fetch, an open or a cross-device guess; a purchase order or an internal customer's order is never attributed. A correction needs MARKETING_CONFIGURE in the seller's organization, the decision read and a reason, and ends the decision rather than changing it. The reports answer a report reader (MARKETING_REPORT_VIEW) of the organizations of their Marketing scope with aggregates only (no person, address or token); an amount of fewer than three orders is withheld unless the reader may see each order in Sales (SalesAccess orderOf) or Commerce, and an attributed order's own row needs the same read; amounts never added across currencies; past a limit TOO_MANY_RECORDS. `count#Visits` and `count#SalesVisits` answer a Marketing reader or a Sales report reader of the organization a count; `count#StoreConversions` checks each store through StoreAdmin. Marketing's datasets (`boss-marketing-deliveries`, `boss-marketing-audience`) carry no recipient, member or address and are scoped by `get#ReportRowFilter` (the ReportScopePolicy MantleMarketingScope: a report reader's Marketing organizations; another userId refused); the audience dataset's records are restricted (a membership's key names its member), its readers get totals. Assist's `marketing_campaign_performance` reads get#CampaignPerformance as the user and `marketing_audience_counts` reads `count#Audience` (bucket and reason counts only, an audience reader of the topic's organization), both Assistant only, never MCP. Retention removes unconfirmed requests, old tracking events (never one an attribution names), expired link tokens and the visitor-key digest of orders older than the organization's statistics retention, and clears purged import rows' names.

Marketing's campaign calendar (`mantle.marketing.MarketingWorkspaceServices` get#CalendarItems, check#CalendarEvent, get#TodayItems, get#CampaignCalendar, all `allow-remote="false"`): dates and names of the campaigns, sends, automations, published content and events of a Marketing reader's organizations (also Home's Upcoming events and publication dates), no recipient, attendee, address or token; a calendar drag moves only an event the scope's calendar holds, through Commons' edit rule. The projected CalDAV collection `projected-MarketingCampaigns` ("Campaign calendar") is read only (a device write refused 403) and admits a holder of MARKETING_VIEW. The shell providers (ironstone.marketing.ContextServices, all `allow-remote="false"`): the palette's `search#Records` (a campaign, list, segment, send, event or registration) and the record-lookup `lookup#Records` (a campaign, send or registration) answer a record only to a user Marketing shows it to (a registration by its id, never its attendee); the call-context `get#CallContext` answers only an events manager, with the ringing number's upcoming registrations, as attendee or buyer, of offers of the user's organizations (at most 20; no address, token or payment); the today-source `get#TodayItems` lists, by the user's groups in the organizations of their scope, the sends due today, the approvals awaiting a decision, the sends with a failed or uncertain delivery and the offers with registrations waiting for the organizer (titles and counts, no recipient, attendee or address); Assist's Log as note and Send as message (`Marketing/LogNote`, `Marketing/SendMessage`) file on a record only after `check#Record` admits the user.

Marketing's event check-in (`mantle.work.EventRegistrationServices` `preview#CheckIn`, `resolve#EntryCode`, `checkIn#Registration` and `get#Attendance`, each `allow-remote="false"`, reached from mr-boss `Marketing/Events/CheckIn` and its `checkIn` and `scanResolve` transitions) is kept by an events manager (MARKETING_EVENT_MANAGE in the offer's organization, another organization's offer answering as a missing one) under their own sign-in, or by an event's shared door tablet: a registered device of the route group MARKETING_CHECKIN, whose allow-list admits CheckIn, its `checkIn` and `scanResolve` transitions alone and whose registration fixes the request parameter `registrationPolicyId` to one offer (an occurrence), its requests running as a display account that holds the bundle BOSS_MARKETING_CHECKIN_DEVICE alone (MARKETING_CHECKIN, no Marketing root grant: the root builds no context for a device and serves it no record panel). `preview#CheckIn`, `resolve#EntryCode` and `checkIn#Registration` accept MARKETING_CHECKIN only in such a device's request (`get#Attendance` is an events manager's alone): the device row's display account is the request's user, its route group names that parameter, its value is the offer asked and the display account belongs to the offer's organization; MARKETING_CHECKIN anywhere else is refused (DEVICE_REQUIRED), another offer answers as a missing one. A device's request is always the tablet's, whatever its display account holds (an events manager's group given to it adds nothing at the device). An entry code is looked up only by its digest (`registrationOfCode`), never stored, put in an address or kept in the session (the scan's answer is a message and the screen loads again empty); an unknown, replaced or revoked code names no one. A place is checked in once, under its row lock (one EventAttendance per registration; a repeat answers the first result), only when it is confirmed, of that offer, its event not cancelled and the door open (a day before the start to twelve hours after the end); an events manager may find an attendee by name or address and check them in with a reason, or override a held or pending place with a reason that changes neither its state nor its payment; the door tablet checks in by code only and is answered the decision, the ticket and the attendee as a first name and initial, never a search, buyer, payment, answers or staff names. Every lookup and check-in counts against the door's limit (60 a minute per device or staff user, the framework's attempt buckets), past which it is refused (RATE_LIMITED) looking nothing up.

A lead form's answer reaches Sales only through Marketing's lead rule, never from the request: once `submitMarketingForm` has kept an answer to a publication of the lead purpose, `mantle.marketing.MarketingFormServices.capture#FormSubmission` runs after the request commits (on a service worker, in a transaction of its own; the page's answer and receipt never wait on it) and the capture job `run_MarketingLeadCaptures` takes up any it missed; each captures the answer once per rule (MarketingLeadRule, a content author's of the publication's organization: MARKETING_CONTENT_EDIT) and `hand#LeadCapture` hands it to Sales' `mantle.sales.OpportunityServices.store#LeadIntake` as the system (`runAsSystem`), which trusts the organization, team and verification a system caller gives. So every value comes from Marketing's records: the organization and campaign from the publication, the team (a current sales team of that organization chosen by a sales manager, SALES_TEAM_MANAGE, or else Sales' own default team, checked when the rule is saved and by Sales at each handoff) and source (offered to it) from the rule, the inquiry's name, organization, email, phone and notes only from the answers to the customer-visible questions published when the answer was submitted that the rule maps (an unmapped, internal or unpublished answer never reaches Sales), and `verified` only when an invitation's address or the signed-in account's address equals the email answered (a typed address identifies nobody). The handoff carries the capture's own operation key, so a repeat (the job, an author's Try again with a reason, a second post under the same request key, which keeps no second answer) answers Sales' first lead; a second answer with the same email joins the open lead as Sales' review item and is never told to the visitor. A failed handoff keeps the answer and records a reason code (never an answer); nothing subscribes anyone. Staff read the rules, captures and counts only through MarketingFormServices (the rules for a content author or the Marketing administrator, the counts for any Marketing reader of the organization), and a capture names its Sales lead only to a caller who can open it in Sales. An event offer's lead rule (source an event registration offer, trigger registered or attended; a content author's of the offer's organization) is captured only by the capture job, as the system (`capture#EventRegistration`, one capture per rule and registration, once a place is confirmed or checked in), and hands Sales the attendee's own name and address (the reservation's only when the attendee is its buyer, never another attendee's), the offer, occurrence and ticket as notes and the registration's campaign, never `verified`.

A Marketing send's test message (`mantle.marketing.CampaignSendServices.test#Send`, MARKETING_SEND_EDIT, the send's organization) goes only to an address of the organization's MarketingPolicy test list (a number of it for a text send; at most ten), checked again at each attempt, at most 20 a day a send, as a promotional ledger delivery from the send's sending profile, so every suppression refuses it; it asks no recipient's consent, is marked TEST and enters no count. A promotional delivery naming a transactional-only sending profile is refused at queue#Delivery.

A Marketing send reaches its recipients only through `mantle.marketing.CampaignSendServices.release#Send` (MARKETING_DISPATCH, the send's organization, a current approval at its revision, a ready promotional sending profile, the policy's audience limit, an address for the unsubscribe link for an email send (a text send carries the opt-out line, and a STOP is its opt-out); the releaser none of its authors nor its approver but under the organization's one-person-team flag with fewer than 500 recipients, recorded) and the due dispatch, which hands only the prepared recipients to the delivery ledger, each checked again just before (the topic's membership, consent, suppression and solicitation, the address prepared, a withdrawal by anyone who holds the address now, the frequency cap) and keyed by the send and its random execution key, so a recipient is handed off once. The ledger checks the recipient's consent within the organization and every suppression at each attempt; each email carries a List-Unsubscribe header and link from a token whose digest alone is kept, under the sending profile's site or a configured root (its relay's web base URL, else the conf webapp's), never a request's Host header. MESSAGE_DELIVERY_REVIEW (ADMIN's and Marketing's dispatcher's) resolves and reads only the deliveries of the reviewer's organizations, every one for a system administrator.

A send's A/B experiment releases its two samples through release#Send as any send's recipients; its remainder reaches recipients only through `mantle.marketing.CampaignSendServices.release#ExperimentWinner` (MARKETING_DISPATCH, the send's organization, a winner chosen with a reason by a dispatcher after the observation period and named by the releaser, the send's current approval at its revision and readiness; the releaser none of its authors nor its approver but under the one-person-team flag with the whole audience under 500, which a paused experiment's resume counts too), once: the remainder is checked against the send's audience evaluated again as the releasing dispatcher and against what the send already sent, so no one new is added and no one gets a second message of it, and each is then checked again by the dispatch just before its hand-off. Any change of the experiment's setup or a variant ends the send's approval.

A Marketing automation reaches people only after `mantle.marketing.CampaignServices.decide#MarketingApproval` (MARKETING_APPROVE, never its author's, the one-person-team flag not applying) and `mantle.marketing.AutomationServices.activate#Automation` (MARKETING_DISPATCH, none of the revision's authors nor its approver, an address for the unsubscribe link). Its admission and step jobs run as its saved owner, the dispatcher who last activated it, signed in for each run and checked again (able to sign in, MARKETING_DISPATCH, the automation's organization, every segment it names readable, Sales and purchase sources under the owner's own access); an owner who lost any of it pauses the automation, and a dispatcher who could not run it cannot activate it. Each send step hands one participant to the delivery ledger through the dispatch's checks (the topic's membership, consent within the organization, suppression and solicitation, a withdrawal by anyone at the address, the frequency cap), once per enrollment and step, an email with the List-Unsubscribe header and link (a text with its opt-out line). The order-placed handler (the SECA OrderPlacedRecordAttribution, `TrackingServices.handle#OrderPlaced`) files only an admission event and the order's attribution, as the system, after the placement commits, each in a transaction of its own.
