# Attack surface: staff applications (mr-boss and Mantle)

The staff applications: mr-boss and the Mantle (mr-mantle) services it reaches. The framework's administration services that BOSS Admin shows are in [Attack-Surface-Admin.md](Attack-Surface-Admin.md). The token routes mr-boss adds (`/s`, `/u`, `/cal`, `/r`, `/i`) and its rate limits are under [Token routes](Attack-Surface-Routes.md#token-routes). The public applications are in [Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS.md](Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS.md). This is one file of the attack surface map. The index, how to read a row, the defaults and the lock-down advice are in [Attack-Surface.md](Attack-Surface.md).

## Access model

mr-boss's `MoquiConf.xml` mounts sixteen app roots under `component://webroot/screen/webroot/apps.xml`, each at `/qapps/<App>`: Commons, Comms, Sales, Marketing, CustomerService, Commerce, Products, Purchasing, Warehouse, Manufacturing, Maintenance, Accounting, People, Projects, Insights and Admin. The file names no property that turns an app off; an app is mounted when the component is loaded. The framework's `BasicComms` item is set `menu-include="false"` there and stays mounted. The same file adds the five token routes ([Token-route screens](#token-route-screens)), two rate limits, and three privileged groups (`BOSS_ADMIN_ACCESS`, `BOSS_ADMIN_BACKUP`, `BOSS_ADMIN_ADMIN`) to the framework's `privileged-group` list.

The grants are seed data: `mr-boss/data/BossSetupData.xml` (`type="seed-initial"`, about 440 authorization rows), `BossAccountingSetupData.xml`, `BossSetupSalesData.xml`, `BossSetupMarketingData.xml` and `BossSetupMaintenanceData.xml`. The rows follow one pattern.

- **App root.** An artifact group holds the root screen (`component://mr-boss/screen/<App>.xml`) with `inheritAuthz="Y"`. A launcher group gets `AUTHZT_ALLOW` on it: `BOSS_USERS` for Commons and Comms, `BOSS_PEOPLE` for People, `BOSS_ACCOUNTING` for Accounting, `BOSS_PROJECT_TEAM` and `BOSS_PROJECT_CLIENT` for Projects. The seed describes `BOSS_ADMIN`, `BOSS_WAREHOUSE`, `BOSS_PURCHASING`, `BOSS_MANUFACTURING` and `BOSS_CUSTOMER_SERVICE` as launcher groups too. The app's administrator group (`BOSS_<APP>_ADMIN`) and the framework's `ADMIN` group each get `AUTHZT_ALWAYS` on it. `BossSetupData.xml` holds 28 `ADMIN` `AUTHZT_ALWAYS` rows.
- **Sub-area.** An artifact group holds an area's screen with `inheritAuthz="Y"`, an `AUTHZT_DENY` row for the launcher group and an `AUTHZT_ALWAYS` row for each role group that works there (for example `BOSS_PPL_PAYROLL`: `People/Payroll.xml`, DENY for `BOSS_PEOPLE`, ALWAYS for `BOSS_PEOPLE_PAYROLL` and `BOSS_PEOPLE_PAYROLL_APPROVE`).
- **Single artifacts.** A group without the launcher gets a non-inheritable ALLOW on named screens, transitions and services (`BOSS_PPL_SELF`, `BOSS_PPL_PAYSLIP_SELF`, `BOSS_PPL_KIOSK`, `BOSS_ADM_SETTINGS_INDEX`).
- **Services by pattern.** Some roots also name services as artifacts with `inheritAuthz="Y"` (`ironstone\.people\..*` on `BOSS_PEOPLE`; `ironstone\.accounting\..*` and eight `mantle.ledger` service files on `BOSS_ACCOUNTING_ACCESS`).

`BOSS_USERS` opens the Commons and Comms roots, Admin's `Admin/Settings/SettingsHome.xml` and `ironstone.admin.ContextServices.get#SharedContext` (both non-inheritable), and is a member of the `BOSS_RECORDS` drive. In `BossSetupData.xml` one row names `ALL_USERS`: `BOSS_PPL_ACCESS_REQUEST_ALL`, an ALLOW on the services `ironstone.admin.LifecycleServices.request#Provisioning` and `request#AccessEnd`.

**The rule** (`crates/moqui-core/src/user/authz.rs`, `is_permitted`). The steps run in this order.

1. When the frame above holds an inheritable `AUTHZT_ALWAYS` for the action, the artifact is permitted and no record is read.
2. Otherwise the user's records for the artifact are read. An `AUTHZT_ALWAYS` record permits at once, whatever DENY record also matches. An `AUTHZT_DENY` record refuses once every record is read. An `AUTHZT_ALLOW` record counts only when no DENY record matched and no frame above was denied. A record whose condition fails to evaluate applies when it is a DENY and does not apply otherwise.
3. With no matching record, an inheritable grant of the frame above permits the artifact when it is not a DENY, is for the same user and covers the action.

An inheritable ALLOW on an app root therefore permits every screen, transition, service and entity operation called below the root, except an artifact that a DENY record names for one of the user's groups. A service called below a root is gated by the checks in its own body (`ec.user.hasPermission`, the scope libraries such as `AccountingPolicy.js` and `SalesAccess.js`), not by an authorization record. A user in both a launcher group and a role group passes a sub-area through the role group's ALWAYS.

**Screen extensions.** `mr-boss/screen-extend/basic-comms/screen/BasicComms/` extends eight framework screens (`Home`, `Chat`, `Calls/Call`, `Calls/Voicemail`, `Inbox/Mail` with eleven transitions, `Inbox/Compose`, `Inbox/MessageDetail`, `Settings/Index`), and `screen-extend/webroot/.../apps/AppList.xml` extends the launcher. The extensions call `ironstone.customerservice.ChannelServices.create#CaseFromMessage`, `ironstone.sales.ContextServices.create#LeadFromMessage` and `ironstone.shared.ChatterServices.search#Users`. They run under the authorization of the screen they extend.

**Demo data.** The files `mr-boss/data/*DemoData.xml` are `type="demo"`. `BossUsersDemoData.xml` holds 144 `currentPassword` attributes and `BossAccountingUsersDemoData.xml` holds 11; the rows read carry the same `SHA` hash value. Twenty-one `*DemoServices.xml` files hold 47 services with `authenticate="anonymous-all"`. No file under `mr-boss/screen`, `mr-boss/screen-extend` or `mr-mantle/service/*.rest.xml` names a `DemoServices` service. The demo data files call them as elements (`<ironstone.commons.DemoServices.load-CommonsDemo/>`), so they run when the `demo` data type is loaded. `BossDemoData.xml` also names `ironstone.commons.DemoServices.approve#DemoPurchase` and `refuse#DemoPurchase` as the services of a demo approval topic.

## Row isolation

Five entity filter sets hang on the authorization rows. How a filter set is evaluated is in [Entity authorization and row filters](Attack-Surface-Data.md#entity-authorization-and-row-filters).

| Set | Defined in | Applies when | Filters |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MANTLE_USER_ORG` | `mr-mantle/data/MantleSetupData.xml:426` | always (no `applyCond`) | By `filterOrgIds`: `Invoice`, `Payment`, `OrderPart`, `ReturnHeader`, `Shipment` (either party), `FinancialAccount`, `AcctgTrans`, `TimePeriod`, `PartyRelationship`, `Party`, `Product`, `ProductCategory`, `ProductFeature`, `ProductFeatureGroup`, `ProductStore`, `Facility`, `UserAccountOrganization`, `InternalOrganization`, `SalesOpportunity`, `SalesForecast`, `Agreement`, `MarketingCampaign`, `MarketSegment`, `ContactList`. |
| `MANTLE_ACTIVE_ORG` | same file, line 491 | `applyCond="activeOrgId"`; `allowMissingAlias="Y"` | The same entities by `activeOrgId`. |
| `MANTLE_PRIVATE_CONTACT` | same file, line 555 | always; `allowMissingAlias="Y"` | `mantle.party.Party`: `privateOwnerUserId` is null or the user's id. |
| `COMMERCE_STORE` | `BossSetupData.xml:2160` | `applyCond="ec.user.context.commerceStoreIds != null"`; `allowMissingAlias="Y"` | `mantle.product.store.ProductStore` by `productStoreId`. |
| `BOSS_PROJECT_CLIENT` | `BossSetupData.xml:328` | always; `allowMissingAlias="N"` | `WorkEffort`, `WorkEffortParty`, `Request`, `CommunicationEvent` and further entities, by the lists `clientProjectIds`, `clientPartyIds`, `clientContactPartyIds` and `clientStatusUpdateIds` of the user context, each `['_NA_']` when unset. |

The two organization sets name no `mantle.humanres.*`, `mantle.work.*`, `mantle.request.*` or `mantle.account.method.*` entity, no order header and no ledger entry. Rows of those entities are limited to an organization only by service code.

**Which rows carry which set.** A launcher ALLOW row carries `MANTLE_USER_ORG` and `MANTLE_PRIVATE_CONTACT`. The `ADMIN` and `BOSS_<APP>_ADMIN` ALWAYS rows on a root carry `MANTLE_ACTIVE_ORG` and `MANTLE_PRIVATE_CONTACT`; `BOSS_PROJECTS_MANAGER` carries `MANTLE_USER_ORG`. With no active organization selected, `MANTLE_ACTIVE_ORG` does not apply, and an administrator's finds are not limited by organization. `COMMERCE_STORE` is attached by `ArtifactAuthzFilter` rows to at least eight Commerce rows (`BOSS_COMMERCE_ADMIN`, `BOSS_COMMERCE_APPADMIN`, `BOSS_COMMERCE_ROOT` and five `BOSS_COM_WEBSITE_*`). `BOSS_PROJECT_CLIENT` is on the client group's ALLOW on the Projects root, in place of the organization set.

**Which record supplies the filters.** `crates/moqui-core/src/user/filter.rs` (`applicable_filters`) takes the filter sets of one record: the record that authorized the frame on top of the artifact stack. A sub-area ALWAYS row becomes that record for the area's screen, and by step 1 of the rule for everything called below it. The sub-area ALWAYS rows read carry no filter set: Accounting's area rows in `BossAccountingSetupData.xml` (the Receivables rows and the single-line rows of the other areas; the file holds no `ArtifactAuthzFilter` row), People's `BOSS_PPL_PAYROLL_OPERATOR`, `BOSS_PPL_PAYROLL_APPROVER` and `BOSS_PPL_HR_AREA_HR`, and Products' `BOSS_PRODUCTS_SETTINGS_ADMIN`. Below those screens no organization or private-contact filter set applies to a find made for a role-group member. For a member of `ADMIN` or of the app's administrator group, step 1 keeps the root's ALWAYS record and its filter sets.

**Where the organization context is set.** Each of the sixteen app root screens calls `mantle.party.PartyServices.setup#UserOrganizationInfo` (`authenticate="anonymous-view"`), in `always-actions` in `Commons.xml`; it is also a method of `mr-mantle/service/mantle.rest.xml`. It writes `userOrgIds`, `filterOrgIds` and `activeOrgId` into `ec.user.context` from `get#PartyOrganizationInfo`, for the signed-in user's own party. It takes no request parameter. The active organization is the user preference `ACTIVE_ORGANIZATION`. A value that is not one of the user's organizations is set to null and the preference is cleared, except for a member of `ADMIN` or a holder of `ACCOUNTING_ADMIN`, who may select any party with the role `OrgInternal`. `filterOrgIds` is the active organization alone when one is set, else every organization of the user (the organizations the party is related to and their direct `PrtOrgRollup` children that are internal). Accounting's `selectOrganization` (`mr-boss/service/ironstone/accounting/Accounting.js`) writes the preference after checking the organization against `policy.access()`. `Commerce.js` `setupStoreScope` writes `commerceStoreIds`: it leaves it unset for a user with no store assignment and for a system administrator, and sets `['_NA_']` when the stores cannot be read. Projects' root calls `mantle.work.ProjectServices.setup#ClientProjectInfo` for the client lists.

**Reads with authorization disabled.** 429 lines under `mr-boss/service` call `disableAuthz` or set `disable-authz`. The files with the most are `projects/BillingServices.xml` (56), `projects/CodeServices.xml` (38), `comms/InboxServices.xml` (31), `projects/TimeServices.xml` (27), `client/ClientServices.xml` (21), `comms/ComposeServices.xml` (20), `commons/PaletteServices.xml` (20) and `shared/RecordFilesServices.xml` (17). The Commons calls read (`CommonsServices.xml`, `ContactServices.xml`, `DriveServices.xml`, `KnowledgeServices.xml`, `TaskServices.xml`) are lookups by key of a `moqui.security.UserAccount` name, a `UserGroup`, a `NotificationTopic` or a work effort's purpose. `shared/ChatterServices.xml` lines 119-136 read the person and employer of an employment, a review and other People records by key with authorization disabled to build a record's title.

## External and device users

**Project clients.** `BOSS_PROJECT_CLIENT` is the group of client contacts with a user account. Its rows in `BossSetupData.xml`: an ALLOW on the Projects root with the `BOSS_PROJECT_CLIENT` and `MANTLE_PRIVATE_CONTACT` filter sets; a DENY on the manager screens (`BOSS_PROJECTS_MANAGERS`: `WorkItems/Import`, `Settings`, `QuickProject`, `NewProject`, `ProjectDetail/Settings`, `Reports/Profitability`, `Reports/Utilization`) and on an internal group (`BOSS_PROJECTS_INTERNAL_CLIENT`); a DENY of create, update and delete on every `mantle\..*` entity (`BOSS_PROJECTS_CLIENT_WRITES`); and a DENY on the `BOSS_COMMONS` and `BOSS_COMMS` artifact groups. A client contact holds no `BOSS_USERS` membership by these rows, so Commons and Comms are closed to it twice. The group holds the permissions `PROJECT_CLIENT` and `EXTERNAL_USER`. A `SubscreensItem` row gives the group the `ClientHours` tab of `ProjectDetail`.

**Shared devices.** A device signs in with the framework's device credential and runs as a display account ([Attack-Surface-Admin.md](Attack-Surface-Admin.md)). Its route group names an artifact group that is the device's allow-list; no authorization row names that group.

| Route group | Allow-list | Fixed parameter | Idle, lifetime | Display account's group |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `INSIGHTS_PLAYLISTS` | `BOSS_DEV_INS_WALL`: `Insights/WallDisplay.xml` and its `data` transition | `definitionId`, required | 10080 minutes, 365 days | `BOSS_INSIGHTS_DISPLAY` (ALLOW on the wall display's page) |
| `PEOPLE_CLOCKING` | `BOSS_DEV_PPL_CLOCKING`: `People/Time/ClockingKiosk.xml`, `punch`, `scanResolve` | none | 10080 minutes, 365 days | `BOSS_PEOPLE_KIOSK`: non-inheritable ALLOW on the same three and on `mantle.humanres.AttendanceServices.record#KioskPunch` and `resolve#KioskBadge`; permission `PEOPLE_KIOSK_PUNCH` |
| `MARKETING_CHECKIN` (`BossSetupMarketingData.xml`) | `BOSS_DEV_MKT_CHECKIN`: `Marketing/Events/CheckIn.xml`, `checkIn`, `scanResolve` | `registrationPolicyId` | was not read | `BOSS_MARKETING_CHECKIN_DEVICE`; permission `MARKETING_CHECKIN` |

A person at the clocking kiosk gives a badge and a PIN. `mr-mantle/service/mantle/humanres/KioskIdentity.js` refuses a caller without `PEOPLE_KIOSK_PUNCH` (line 458) and compares the PIN with the badge's `pinHash` through `ec.user.passwordsMatch` (line 626). `mr-mantle/service/mantle/party/SharedDevices.js` (`requireAny`) refuses a device administration call from a caller who is not signed in or holds none of the named permissions.

**Invitations.** The services are `mantle.party.IdentityServices` over `mr-mantle/service/mantle/party/Invitations.js`. The token is 43 characters from `ec.user.randomString`; the row keeps `ec.user.singleUseCodeHash` of it. The expiry is `expireDays` from the issue, 7 by default, held between 1 and 30. `invitationRefusal` refuses `ADMIN` and every privileged group as a bundle. At acceptance `inviterRefusal` checks the inviter again from the records: the account is usable, it holds `ADMIN_IDENTITY_MANAGE`, the organization is internal and is one the inviter works for (any for a member of `ADMIN`), and each bundle is registered, enabled and, when it has a `delegablePermissionId`, held by the inviter. `acceptInvitation` locks the row and refuses any step but `invitation` with one message (`This invitation is no longer valid`). A signed-in caller must be the account whose email address is the invited one. A caller who is not signed in is refused when an account already has the address; otherwise the service creates a person and a user account with the username and password the caller sends, an organization relationship (`PrtEmployee` by default) and the group memberships, the last with authorization enabled.

## Token-route screens

The five routes are declared in mr-boss's `MoquiConf.xml`; each names a token service that runs before the screen. The route table and the token rules are under [Token routes](Attack-Surface-Routes.md#token-routes). Every screen has `require-authentication="false"`. Each transition passes the route's `token` to its service. When a transition runs on a GET and when the session token is checked is in [CSRF](Attack-Surface-Web.md#csrf); a row below that says "no `method`" runs on a POST and on a GET that has no query string.

| Path | Method, session token | Service | Caller controls |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/s/<token>/open` | no `method`; `require-session-token="false"` | `org.moqui.resource.FileServices.open#PublicShare` | `password`. Answers `valid`, `passwordOk`, `grant`, `share`. |
| `/s/<token>/files` | GET, `read-only` | `FileServices.list#PublicShare` | `grant`, `path` |
| `/s/<token>/download` | GET, `read-only` | `FileServices.download#PublicShare` (sends the file or a zip of a folder) | `grant`, `path`, `inline`. The grant is a query parameter. |
| `/s/<token>/upload` | no `method`; `require-session-token="false"` | `FileServices.upload#PublicShare` | `grant`, `path`, `contentFile` (one or several files), `uploaderName` |
| `/u/<token>/state` | GET, `read-only` | `mantle.party.ConsentServices.resolve#UnsubscribeToken` | nothing. Answers the organization and list names, the masked address, the status and the party's other lists. |
| `/u/<token>/unsubscribe` | no `method`; `require-session-token="false"` | `ConsentServices.unsubscribe#ContactListParty` (`anonymous-all`) | `choice` (`all` for all marketing email), `reasonText`, `verifyCode` |
| `/u/<token>/preferences` | no `method`; `require-session-token="false"` | `ConsentServices.apply#UnsubscribePreferences` (`anonymous-all`) | `listChoices` (JSON list of `contactListId`, `subscribed`), `reasonText` |
| `/u/<token>` (POST, the route's `post-transition`) and `/u/<token>/oneClick` | POST; `require-session-token="false"` | `unsubscribe#ContactListParty` with `listUnsubscribe: 'One-Click'`, in its own transaction | The form field `List-Unsubscribe` must be `One-Click`, else 400. A refused token answers 410. A GET of `/u/<token>/oneClick` redirects to the page. |
| `/cal/<token>/events` | GET, `read-only` | `ironstone.commons.CalendarServices.get#PublishedCalendar` | `password`, `start`, `end`. The page sends the password as a query parameter. |
| `/r/<token>/submit` | POST; `require-session-token="false"` | `mantle.request.RequestServices.create#IntakeRequest` | `firstName`, `lastName`, `emailAddress`, `organizationName`, `requestTypeEnumId`, `requestName`, `description`, `website`, `reCaptchaToken`, `contentFile`. Rate limit `request-form`: 5 POSTs in 60 seconds per client on `/r/*/submit`. |
| `/i/<token>` | GET (the page) | `mantle.party.IdentityServices.get#InvitationSummary` | `stage`. Shows the inviter's name, the organization, the invited address, the bundles, the expiry, and whether an account has the address. |
| `/i/<token>/acceptExisting` | no `method`; no `require-session-token` attribute, so the default applies | `IdentityServices.accept#Invitation` | nothing but the token; the signed-in account is the subject |
| `/i/<token>/createAccount` | no `method`; default session-token rule | `IdentityServices.accept#Invitation` | `username`, `userFullName`, `newPassword`, `newPasswordVerify` |
| `/i/<token>/confirmFactor` | no `method`; default session-token rule | `IdentityServices.confirm#InvitationFactor` | `code`. Rate limit `invitation-accept`: 10 POSTs in 60 seconds per client on `/i/*/*`. |

The page render of `/u/<token>` and `/s/<token>` calls no changing service. A GET of `/u/<token>/unsubscribe` with no query string calls `unsubscribe#ContactListParty` for the token's list with `choice` unset. For a token call that is neither one-click nor by mail the service sets `confirmed` false (`ConsentServices.xml` line 1101). A GET of `/u/<token>/preferences` with no query string sends an empty list. A GET of `/s/<token>/open` with no query string calls `open#PublicShare` with no password. A GET of `/i/<token>/acceptExisting` with no query string accepts the invitation for the signed-in account whose address is the invited one. The unsubscribe token is 16 to 128 characters of `[A-Za-z0-9_-]`, looked up by its hash in `ContactListToken`, with its `thruDate` checked.

## Admin

Admin's screens show the framework's administration services, which are in [Attack-Surface-Admin.md](Attack-Surface-Admin.md), and Mantle's identity services (`mr-mantle/service/mantle/party/Identity.js`, `Invitations.js`, `SharedDevices.js`). The launcher group is `BOSS_ADMIN`. The role groups and their permissions, from `BossSetupData.xml`:

- `BOSS_ADMIN_READ`: `ADMIN_OVERVIEW_VIEW`.
- `BOSS_ADMIN_IDENTITY`: `ADMIN_IDENTITY_VIEW`, `ADMIN_IDENTITY_MANAGE`, and owner-change permissions (among them `DRIVE_OWNER_CHANGE`, `EMAIL_ACCOUNT_OWNER_CHANGE`, `CALENDAR_OWNER_CHANGE`, `CONTACT_BOOK_OWNER_CHANGE`, `REPORT_OWNER_CHANGE`, `PEOPLE_APPROVAL_OWNER_CHANGE`).
- `BOSS_ADMIN_ACCESS` (privileged): `ADMIN_ACCESS_VIEW`, `ADMIN_ACCESS_MANAGE`.
- `BOSS_ADMIN_CONFIGURE`: `ADMIN_SETTINGS_VIEW`, `ADMIN_SETTINGS_MANAGE`, `ORG_CONSTITUTION_EDIT`.
- `BOSS_ADMIN_INTEGRATIONS`: `ADMIN_CONNECTION_VIEW`, `ADMIN_CONNECTION_MANAGE`.
- `BOSS_ADMIN_OPERATIONS`: `ADMIN_OPERATION_VIEW`, `ADMIN_OPERATION_MANAGE`.
- `BOSS_ADMIN_DATA`: `ADMIN_TRANSFER_VIEW`, `ADMIN_TRANSFER_RUN`.
- `BOSS_ADMIN_AUDIT`: `ADMIN_AUDIT_VIEW`.
- `BOSS_ADMIN_BACKUP` (privileged): `ADMIN_BACKUP_VIEW`, `ADMIN_BACKUP_RUN`.
- `BOSS_ADMIN_ADMIN` (privileged): the sixteen `ADMIN_*` view, manage and run permissions of the groups above, `DRIVE_OWNER_CHANGE` and further owner-change permissions.

The area artifact groups are `BOSS_ADM_OPERATIONS`, `BOSS_ADM_AGENTS`, `BOSS_ADM_SYSTEM`, `BOSS_ADM_ACCESS`, `BOSS_ADM_USERS`, `BOSS_ADM_ORGANIZATIONS`, `BOSS_ADM_SECURITY`, `BOSS_ADM_INTEGRATIONS`, `BOSS_ADM_PROTOCOLS`, `BOSS_ADM_DATA`, `BOSS_ADM_DATA_BACKUPS` and `BOSS_ADM_AUDIT`. `BOSS_ADM_SETTINGS_INDEX` opens `Admin/Settings/SettingsHome.xml` to every `BOSS_USERS` member without the Admin root. `Identity.js` names `ADMIN_IDENTITY_VIEW`, `ADMIN_IDENTITY_MANAGE` and `ADMIN_IDENTITY_GLOBAL` as its permissions. The `AgentToolExposure` row `admin_get_readiness` exposes `org.moqui.admin.ReadinessServices.get#Readiness` as an agent tool.

People's hire and separation reach Admin's lifecycle through `ironstone.admin.LifecycleServices.request#Provisioning` and `request#AccessEnd` (`mr-boss/service/ironstone/admin/Provisioning.js`). The first requires an `EmploymentChange` of kind `EckHire` with `accessRequested` `Y`. Its proposal is `BOSS_USERS` and `BOSS_PEOPLE_SELF`, plus each bundle of `bundleGroupIdList` that is registered, enabled, not privileged and, when the bundle has a `delegablePermissionId`, one whose permission the caller holds. A bundle with no `delegablePermissionId` passes this check for any caller. The request then goes to `mantle.party.IdentityServices.request#Provisioning` as the caller.

## Commons

The Commons root is open to every `BOSS_USERS` member, with `MANTLE_USER_ORG` and `MANTLE_PRIVATE_CONTACT`. Commons holds files and drives, contacts, calendars, tasks, knowledge spaces, requests and the notification inboxes.

`ironstone.commons.ContactServices.check#ContactAccess` reads the party and treats it as visible when it has no `privateOwnerUserId` or the owner is the caller. An edit needs the owner for a personal contact, or a contact administrator (`CONTACT_ADMIN`, the group `BOSS_CONTACT_ADMIN` or `ADMIN`) for a shared one. A holder of `SALES_ACCOUNT_EDIT` may edit the identity fields of a person who has no user account and is a contact of an account in their Sales scope. A delete of a shared contact needs a contact administrator, no user account on the party and no `OrgInternal` role. The find is by `partyId` with authorization on, so the `Party` filters of the authorizing record apply; the service adds no organization condition of its own. `ContactServices.migrate#NotDuplicateSettings` is `anonymous-all` and runs with authorization disabled over every `BossNotDuplicates` party setting.

## People

The launcher group is `BOSS_PEOPLE`; its ALLOW covers the People root and `ironstone\.people\..*` services. The role groups and permissions: `BOSS_PEOPLE_READ` (`PEOPLE_DIRECTORY_VIEW`), `BOSS_PEOPLE_SELF` (`PEOPLE_SELF_SERVICE`), `BOSS_PEOPLE_MANAGER` (`PEOPLE_TEAM_VIEW`, `PEOPLE_REQUEST_APPROVE`, `PEOPLE_ROSTER_EDIT`), `BOSS_PEOPLE_HR` (`PEOPLE_HR_VIEW`, `PEOPLE_HR_EDIT`, `PEOPLE_IMPORT`, `PEOPLE_REQUEST_APPROVE`, `PEOPLE_ROSTER_EDIT`, `PEOPLE_EXPENSE_MANAGE`), `BOSS_PEOPLE_RECRUIT` (`PEOPLE_RECRUIT_VIEW`, `PEOPLE_RECRUIT_EDIT`), `BOSS_PEOPLE_PAYROLL` (`PEOPLE_DIRECTORY_VIEW`, `PAYROLL_VIEW`, `PAYROLL_EDIT`, `PAYROLL_CONFIGURE`, `PEOPLE_IMPORT`), `BOSS_PEOPLE_PAYROLL_APPROVE` (`PEOPLE_DIRECTORY_VIEW`, `PAYROLL_VIEW`, `PAYROLL_APPROVE`), `BOSS_PEOPLE_DEVELOP` (`PEOPLE_DEVELOP_VIEW`, `PEOPLE_DEVELOP_EDIT`), `BOSS_PEOPLE_CASE` (`PEOPLE_CASE_HANDLE`), `BOSS_PEOPLE_ADMIN` (`PAYROLL_VIEW`, `PAYROLL_EDIT`, `PAYROLL_CONFIGURE`, `PEOPLE_CONFIGURE`, `PEOPLE_ALL_ORGANIZATIONS`). Among the `BOSS_PEOPLE*` groups only `BOSS_PEOPLE_PAYROLL_APPROVE` holds `PAYROLL_APPROVE`, and it does not hold `PAYROLL_EDIT`.

**Payroll.** `People/Payroll.xml` and what is below it open to the payroll operator and the payroll approver (ALWAYS) and are denied to the launcher group. In `mr-mantle/service/mantle/humanres/PayrollRuns.js` a read needs the `payrollView` capability, a preparation needs `PAYROLL_VIEW` and `PAYROLL_EDIT`, and `runIn` refuses a run whose `organizationPartyId` is not in the caller's People scope.
