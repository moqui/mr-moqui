# Attack surface: PASS commerce and portals

The public applications that sell, bill and serve customers and suppliers: Shop, Billing, Supplier, Support, PASS Sales and PASS Marketing, as mr-pass pages and the Mantle services behind them. How every PASS page reaches the authority, public accounts and sites are in [Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS.md](Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS.md); Forum and Smithy are in [Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS-Forum-Smithy.md](Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS-Forum-Smithy.md). This is one file of the attack surface map. The index, how to read a row, the defaults and the lock-down advice are in [Attack-Surface.md](Attack-Surface.md).

## Shop

PASS Shop is `mantle.order.ShopCartServices`, `mantle.order.ShopCheckoutServices`, `mantle.party.ShopCustomerServices`, `mantle.product.ShopFulfillmentServices`, `mantle.product.ShopSubscriptionServices`, the review services of `service/mantle/product/StoreReviews.js` and the theme reads of `mantle.public.ThemeServices`, each service `authenticate="anonymous-all"`. The store comes from the site's enabled `shop` mount (`service/mantle/product/ShopContext.js` `shopOf`: a published site, a store of an internal organization), never from the request.

### Cart

`mantle.order.ShopCartServices` (PASS operations `getCart`, `addCartItem`, `updateCartItem`, `removeCartItem`, `applyPromoCode`, `removePromoCode`, `attachCart`, `releaseCart` and `recoverCart`; `service/mantle/order/ShopCart.js`). A cart is an open `OrderHeader` with a `StoreCartAccess` row. The caller is the holder of the cart capability (`input.token`, 48 random characters, only its digest stored, kept by the frontend in its server session) or the signed-in account, whose own cart is found by its party. No operation takes an order id. A capability reaches a cart only on the same site, store and currency, while the row is unrevoked and unexpired and the order open; a cart attached to an account is reached only by that account. Each capability lookup counts against the requester (600 in ten minutes). The first `addCartItem` with no cart makes one: a guest `Party`, `Person` and `PartyRole`, an order and the access row, limited to 30 carts a requester in ten minutes and, for a guest's cart, 1,000 a site an hour (`cart-site:`, SEC-PSS-05). The request gives a product, an option, a quantity (1 to 999), a line id, a code, the revision seen and an operation id. The unit price is the authority's (`offersOf`) at each add and change. A cart holds 50 lines. A promo code is tried at most 20 times a requester in ten minutes and every refusal reads the same. `attachCart` needs a sign-in, attaches or merges the session's guest cart and rotates the capability. `releaseCart` rotates the capability of every open cart of the account on the site. `recoverCart` (`service/mantle/order/CartRecovery.js`) takes a one-time token (48 characters, its digest on the access row; mailed as `/Recover?recoveryToken=`) and needs the signed-in account whose cart it is; a visitor is answered 401 and the token is kept; lookups are limited to 120 a requester in ten minutes. A cart with a payment in progress or a submitted checkout is not changed.

### Checkout and payment

`mantle.order.ShopCheckoutServices` (thirteen PASS operations, `beginCheckout` to `getOrderConfirmation`, all in `TOKEN_WRITES`; `service/mantle/order/ShopCheckout.js`). One `StoreCheckout` row coordinates a cart to one placement. Every write needs the cart's holder; a call reached any other way is NotFound. After submission the checkout is also read by its reference (the `checkoutId`) with a return key (40 random characters, made at each submit, only its digest kept, valid while the checkout is open and for a day after it completes), by the capability of a cart placed less than a day ago, or by the signed-in customer with the reference. The reference and key read a minimal view (the state, the amount due, the attempt's state and provider address) and the order's id, date and status; they are given no contact, address, line or order link. The request gives contact fields, address fields or one of the account's own addresses, a delivery option of the current quote, a payment option, the revision and terms version seen, opt-ins and an operation id. No price, total, tax, delivery charge, customer or status is read from the request. `submitCheckout` re-quotes delivery, then under the order's lock checks every line's price and availability, the promotion limits, the quote, the tax and the terms; a change answers `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and nothing is charged. The payment's amount is the order's total. A total of zero or less skips the gateway. The gateway is called through `authorize#SinglePayment` with a return address on the site's canonical host and Shop mount (`/Checkout/return?ref=…&key=…`), never a caller's origin. The payment's state is read from the `Payment` row's status, which only the gateway lookup (`get#SinglePaymentGatewayDetails`), the provider's webhook and the reconciliation job change; nothing the browser's return brings back is read. A result read asks the provider at most once a second. `place#Order` runs once the payment is approved, only when the order's revision and total are still the frozen ones. `getCheckoutPaymentForm` (fields `siteId`, `reference`, `key`) answers a fresh Authorize.Net Accept Hosted form address and token for a pending attempt whose amount equals the frozen amount; the token is not stored. A guest's order link (`orderToken`, the `SalesOrder` kind's `OrderView`, with `Download` for a digital order, 366 days) is issued once, only to a call that came by the cart's own capability. Stored operation results leave out the return key, the order token and the cart token.

### Relay and result pages of mr-pass

`screen/Shop/PaymentRelay.xml`, `MethodRelay.xml` and `PaymentResult.xml` are `require-authentication="false"` and run their script in `pre-actions`. `PaymentRelay` (`ShopCheckout.js` `relayPage`) reads `ref` (cut to 40 characters of `[A-Za-z0-9_-]`), calls `getCheckoutPaymentForm` with that reference and the key the session keeps for it, and writes a form whose `action` is the answered `formUrl` and whose hidden fields are the answered fields, each escaped; the page's same-origin script (`Checkout/enhance`) submits it. The checkout relay does not itself check the host of `formUrl`; the route's CSP `form-action` does. `MethodRelay` (`ShopAccount.js` `relayPage`) reads the form the session kept from `beginPaymentMethodSetup`, removes it from the session, and writes it only when its `formUrl` starts `https://accept.authorize.net/` or `https://test.authorize.net/`; otherwise it redirects to `PaymentMethods`. `PaymentResult` has one transition, `state` (`method="get"`, `read-only="true"`), which answers the checkout's state for the reference and the session's key. The pages are `no-store` and cannot be framed (`frame-ancestors 'none'`, see the headers above). `PaymentMethods/add` and `PaymentMethods/end` are `method="post"`; `PaymentMethods/return` has no `method`.

### Orders and the guest order link

`mantle.party.ShopCustomerServices` (fourteen services; `service/mantle/party/ShopCustomer.js`). An order is reached by a guest's order link (`input.orderToken`, the `SalesOrder` `OrderView` capability, its one order; mailed and long-lived, and a write credential: it also files a cancellation, which applies at once under `customerImmediateCancel`, a return and a case in the customer's name, SEC-PSS-23; which must be of the site's store; an `orderId` beside it must be its own) or by the signed-in account in the self relationship, for an order of the store whose every part has the account's party as customer and the store's organization as vendor. Another's order and an unknown one are the same NotFound. `findShopOrders`, `getShopOrder` and `getShopOrderDocument` are the link's reads; `getShopOrderDocument` answers only an invoice that is one of the reached order's. `previewReorder` and `reorder` drop the link and need the account. A verified account's first read merges into its party each guest party of a completed guest checkout of the store whose typed contact email equals the account's email (`mantle.party.DuplicateServices.merge#Parties`), when that party is still only the guest of that one cart.

### Addresses and payment methods

`get#Addresses`, `store#Address`, `end#Address`, `get#PaymentMethods`, `begin#PaymentMethodSetup`, `finish#PaymentMethodSetup` and `end#PaymentMethod` each need a signed-in account in the self relationship (`requireSelf`); a signed-in account outside it is `Unauthorized`. `store#Address` and `begin`/`finish#PaymentMethodSetup` first count against the requester, 30 in ten minutes (`shop-account:`, SEC-PSS-67). `store#Address` takes a name, two address lines, a city, a postal code, a country and a state (`address1` at most 255 characters, `city` 100, `postalCode` 60; the country must be a `moqui.basic.Geo` of type `GEOT_COUNTRY`, the state any existing `Geo`) and a purpose of billing or shipping. A change names a `contactMechId`, which must be a postal address with a current `PartyContactMech` of the caller's party, and the revision shown. An account keeps at most `limits.addresses` addresses. The store's carrier validation (`mantle.shipment.CarrierServices.validate#PostalAddress`) is called when the store has a shipping gateway with one; its suggestion is answered and not applied. `end#Address` ends only the caller's party's own purposes of that address. `begin#PaymentMethodSetup` calls the store gateway's hosted setup service with the caller's party, a 20-character reference and a return address made from the site's Shop mount (`/PaymentMethods/return?setup=…`), and answers the provider's redirect address or form. `finish#PaymentMethodSetup` calls the gateway's setup sync service with the caller's party and the request's `setupReference`. `end#PaymentMethod` ends only a method whose `ownerPartyId` is the caller's party and that is not ended; when recurring orders use it the call needs `confirm`, and each contract that renewed with it loses its consent. A card is entered on the gateway's hosted page; the list shows the brand, last four digits and expiry. These functions make no rate-limit call of their own.

### Subscriptions

`mantle.product.ShopSubscriptionServices` `get#Subscriptions`, `get#Subscription`, `begin#SubscriptionPurchase`, `change#SubscriptionMethod`, `cancel#Subscription` and `resume#Renewal` each need a signed-in account in the self relationship and a contract whose store is the site's and whose `customerPartyId` is the account's party; anything else is NotFound. A write names the contract's revision. A new card for renewals must be one of the account's own.

### Digital downloads

`mantle.product.ShopFulfillmentServices` `get#Downloads`, `issue#Download` and `get#DownloadFile` (PASS operations `getDownloads`, `issueDownload`, `getShopDownload`). A grant (`StoreDownloadGrant`) is reached by an order link with the `Download` scope (its one order, with a completed checkout on this site) or by the signed-in verified account (its own grants in the store). `issueDownload` is limited to 60 a requester in ten minutes and makes a `SalesOrder` capability of scope `DownloadFile` for the grant's order that lasts ten minutes, which `getShopDownload` alone accepts: it is no order link and issues no successor (SEC-PSS-03). `getShopDownload` takes the grant id and that token, checks the capability against the grant's order and the store's organization, checks the grant again (unrevoked, in its dates, its fulfillment completed), counts the download and answers the file's location, which is the `contentLocation` of the grant's `ProductContent` row (type `PcntDownload`), and only when that location is under the content root in the product's own folder (`ShopFulfillment.js` `servedDownload` with `Content.js` `owned`: `dbresource://mantle/content` or the `mantle.content.root` preference, `product/<productId>/`, no `..`); any other answers NOT_AVAILABLE. `create#ProductContent` and `Content.js` `update` refuse such a location for a download row; the entity-auto `create#mantle.product.ProductContent` (mr-marble `createContentWithLocation`) still stores any location, which nothing serves as a download (SEC-PSS-01, fixed). The frontend streams it with authorization off, as an attachment with `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Cache-Control: no-store`, a 1 MiB window at a time, honouring one `bytes=start-end` range. The bundled frontend issues the token and streams in one request, so the token is not sent to the browser.

### Product images and reviews

`getShopProductImage` answers a `ProductContent` image of a product public in the store now, only when its `contentLocation` starts `dbresource://` or `component://`, has no `..`, backslash or encoded dot or slash, and ends in png, jpg, jpeg, gif or webp; the content type is the extension's. It is `public, max-age=60` only when the request names the site's current generation. `getShopReviewImage` answers a review image by a digest (16 to 128 characters) only while its review is approved, of the site's store, and its product public; the content type is the stored `image/png` or `image/jpeg`. `submitProductReview` counts against the requester first (30 in ten minutes), then needs a signed-in account with a verified email and, for a first review, a delivered order line of the product; an edit must name the caller's own review. The rating is 1 to 5 and the body at most 5,000 characters of plain text. A photo is staged only after those checks. It must be declared `image/png` or `image/jpeg`, at most three a review, and goes through `service/mantle/party/PortalMedia.js` `stage` (the rules under [Supplier](#supplier)): at most 10,485,760 bytes, the leading bytes must be the declared type's, and the scanner must answer clean. It is kept under the content root at `productReview/{store}/{review}/{seq}.{png|jpg}`, a path made from ids. A new or edited review is `PrvwPending` until staff approve it.

### Themes

A store's theme is a package a Commerce user writes (`COMMERCE_THEME_EDIT`) and publishes (`COMMERCE_THEME_PUBLISH`) in the stores of the user's scope. It is rendered on the public Shop pages of the site (the page kinds home, not-found, category, product, search, page, cart and checkout).

- **The package.** `service/mantle/public/Themes.js` takes paths that match `^[A-Za-z0-9_-][A-Za-z0-9_.-]*(/[A-Za-z0-9_-][A-Za-z0-9_.-]*)*$`, text files ending `.json`, `.html.fancy-locked`, `.css` or `.svg`, and binary files ending `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`, `.webp` or `.woff2`: at most 300 files, 512 KiB a file and 8 MiB in all. The renderer's own bounds (`crates/moqui-core/src/script/theme/package.rs`) are 2,000 files, 1 MiB a text file, 1 MiB an assembled template and 16 MiB a binary asset named by reference. The renderer takes the package as text; a binary asset is only a reference (`{digest, size, contentType}`) and its bytes are not part of a render. The layout is the whole HTML document (the starter's `layouts/store.html.fancy-locked` begins `<html>` and `<head>`).
- **Expressions.** Each `${}` expression is parsed and checked before any runs (`expr.rs`). A denied name is refused as a variable or a property: `ec`, `sri`, `context`, `raw`, `globalThis`, `eval`, `Function`, `AsyncFunction`, `GeneratorFunction`, `constructor`, `__proto__`, `prototype`, the `__define*__` and `__lookup*__` names, `defineProperty`, `defineProperties`, `setPrototypeOf`, `getPrototypeOf`, `Reflect`, `Proxy`, `Symbol`, `arguments`, `require`, `import`, `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest`, `WebSocket`, `setTimeout`, `setInterval`, `queueMicrotask`, `Atomics`, `SharedArrayBuffer`, `WebAssembly`, and any name that starts with `$`. A computed property is checked only when it is a string literal or a template literal with no expression; a property name built at run time (`x["con" + "structor"]`) is not seen by this check. `this`, `new`, a function, a class, an assignment, `++` and `--`, `import`, `yield`, `await`, a tagged template, `super`, `delete` and an arrow function with a block body are refused. Each layout, section and block runs through `template::render_locked_bounded` (`compose.rs`), with the view as a JSON snapshot whose values are the template's names, a global object frozen with everything reachable from it (`crates/moqui-core/src/script/template.rs` `SANDBOX_LOCKDOWN`), and that renderer's own ceilings of 5 seconds and 32 MiB.
- **Markup.** Template source is checked before it runs and each render's output is checked again (`markup.rs`, modes `Source` and `Output`). Refused elements: `script`, `iframe`, `frame`, `frameset`, `object`, `embed`, `applet`, `form`, `base`, `portal`, `noscript`, `xmp`, `plaintext`, `noembed`, `noframes`, `math`, `isindex`, `keygen`, `fencedframe`, and the SVG elements `foreignobject`, `animate`, `animatemotion`, `animatetransform`, `set`, `discard`, `handler`, `listener`, and a `style` inside an SVG. Refused attributes: any whose name starts with `on`, and `srcdoc`, `formaction`, `formmethod`, `formenctype`, `formtarget`, `formnovalidate`, `form`, `action`, `ping`, `http-equiv` and `is`. A `<link>` must have a `rel` of `stylesheet`, `icon`, `apple-touch-icon`, `preload`, `canonical`, `alternate`, `next`, `prev`, `author` or `license`. A tag inside `<title>` or `<textarea>`, an unclosed `<style>`, `<title>` or `<textarea>`, an `<svg>` left open in a render's output, and markup that ends inside a tag are refused. The deny lists do not hold `meta`, `link`, `style`, `input`, `button`, `select`, `img`, `video` or `audio`.
- **URLs and CSS.** The `href` of `a` and `area` (and of a `link` with a navigation `rel`) may be a relative address or an `http`, `https`, `mailto` or `tel` address of any host. A resource address (`src`, `poster`, `data`, `background`, `cite`, `manifest`, `srcset`, a stylesheet's `href`) may be relative, on an approved origin, or a `data:` PNG, GIF, JPEG, WebP or AVIF image; any other scheme is refused. A `style` attribute, a `<style>` element and SVG paint attributes that hold `url` are checked as CSS, which refuses `@import`, `expression(`, `javascript:`, `vbscript:`, `-moz-binding`, `</style`, `<!--`, `-->`, `image-set(`, `src(` and the `behavior` properties. The approved origins are the render request's `allowedOrigins`; mr-pass's render call (`screen/Shop/ShopTheme.js` `renderView`) passes none.
- **Limits.** A page is bounded to 100 sections, 100 blocks, composition depth 8, include depth 8, 2 MiB of output, one second a render (a layout, a section or a block), and the locked renderer's page time and memory. The `theme-limits` element of the conf can lower each value, never raise it.
- **What a theme sees.** `get#ShopPage` answers the theme an allow-listed view (`service/mantle/public/ThemeView.js` `contract()`: the fields its `shapes` name, dropping any name that is or ends in secret, token, password, partyid or userid). The cart's and checkout's views carry the store's data only. The frontend makes trusted component HTML from the page's view (`ShopTheme.js` `COMPONENTS`), which the renderer places by placeholder; a placeholder that is not one of the render's own, or that stands in an attribute, a comment, a `<style>` or an `<svg>`, is refused. The components given are those of the page kind, and when the release's `theme.json` has a `requiredComponents` list only the ones it names. A render that fails answers a fixed 503 page; diagnostics go to the log.
- **Reads and assets.** `mantle.public.ThemeServices` `get#ShopThemeRelease`, `get#ShopThemeFiles` and `get#ShopThemeAsset` (`CLIENT_READS`) answer only the release that is the site's Shop mount's active one (`RELEASE_NOT_ACTIVE`, NotFound, for another id). `getShopThemeFiles` takes 1 to 50 paths and answers only files the release pins. `getShopThemeAsset` answers only a binary path the release pins, named with the digest it pins; the bytes at the pinned version are checked against that digest before the location is answered; the type is the extension's (`image/png`, `image/jpeg`, `image/gif`, `image/webp`, `font/woff2`). The frontend serves an asset at `{mount}/theme/{manifestDigest}/{path}` (`sendAsset`): the digest must be the active release's, the path must match `^assets/[A-Za-z0-9_-]+(/[A-Za-z0-9_.-]+)*\.[A-Za-z0-9]+$` with no `..`, and the extension must be one of its asset types, so template source is never served. An SVG is passed through `ec.resource.theme.sanitizeSvg` when served. An asset answer carries `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; img-src 'self' data:`.
- **Previews.** A draft preview (`create#ThemePreview`) lasts twenty minutes; its token is 40 letters and digits and only its digest is stored. `get#ThemePreviewPage` (`anonymous-all`, `service/mantle/public/ThemeAuthoring.js`) answers the draft's files and view only for a token of 20 to 80 letters and digits that is unexpired, of the request's site, on a host of kind `PshkPreview` of that site, whose maker's account exists and is not disabled; anything else is NotFound. The function makes no rate-limit call of its own.

## Billing

### Invoices and their payment

`mantle.account.PortalServices` `find#Invoices`, `get#Invoice`, `issue#InvoiceAccess`, `start#InvoicePayment` and `get#PaymentResult` (PASS operations `findInvoices`, `getInvoice`, `issueInvoiceAccess`, `startInvoicePayment`, `getPaymentResult`; `service/mantle/account/Portal.js`), each `authenticate="anonymous-all"`. Each finds the organization from the published site's `billing` mount. `getInvoice` and `startInvoicePayment` take an `InvoicePay` token of an invoice of that organization, or a signed-in account that is the invoice's customer (self) or a Billing contact of it; an invoice id beside a token must be the token's. `findInvoices` needs a sign-in. `issueInvoiceAccess` from a page answers only a Billing contact, never a token or the self relationship: it revokes the invoice's `InvoicePay` links whose issuer kind is `contact` and makes a new one for 120 days, whose token is answered once. `startInvoicePayment` is the one bearer write (`TOKEN_WRITES`). It, a contact's `issueInvoiceAccess` and `submitSupplierBill` count first against the requester, 60 posts in ten minutes (`billing-post:`, SEC-PSS-26). The amount is the invoice's stored unpaid total, never a request value; a pending attempt is resumed. It makes a `Payment` (`PmntPromised`) and a `PortalPaymentAttempt` (purpose `PapInvoice`) with a 40-character return key whose digest alone is kept, and gives the gateway the return address `/InvoicePay/return?attemptId=…&returnToken=…` made from the site's Billing mount. `getPaymentResult` answers the attempt's state and the invoice for the invoice's token or contact; for the return key (`returnToken`, or `returnKey`) it answers only `{attemptId, state}` (paid, declined, pending or failed), with no amount, date, payment reference, provider page or invoice: the key reads only its own attempt, on the site it was started from, while the invoice's record is still of the mount's organization, and for a limited time after the attempt settled (`RETURN_READ_MS`). PASS `Billing/InvoicePay.xml` reads the attempt with the pay link (the page's or the session's) or the sign-in first, and otherwise shows the state alone with a note to open the invoice email's link or sign in. The outcome is read from the gateway (`mantle.account.PaymentServices.get#SinglePaymentGatewayDetails`) and an authorized payment is captured there (`capture#SinglePayment`); nothing the browser returns is read. A read of a pending attempt makes that gateway lookup. A pending attempt that failed without an answer (`STARTING_MS`, five minutes; `ABANDONED_MS`, seven days) is cancelled when a new one starts. The answered redirect address is given only when it starts `https://` or one `/`. The mr-pass page keeps the link's token for the return in the session attribute `passPayToken.{attemptId}`. The same file has `begin#InvoicePayment`, `refresh#PortalPayment`, `resolve#PortalPayments`, `check#AppReadiness` and `get#AccountCard`, also `anonymous-all`; whether a PASS route names them was not checked.

### Payment callbacks and browser card tokens

- **Webhooks.** `mantle.ext.account.gateway.AuthorizeNetServices.receive#Webhook` and `mantle.ext.shipment.ShippoServices.receive#Webhook` run after `/rest/sm/{type}/{remote}` has checked the remote's signature (see [REST](Attack-Surface-Routes.md#rest)). Authorize.Net's keeps an event once per remote and `notificationId`. Its consume step reads only the transaction id and `merchantReferenceId` of the payload, finds the payments of the gateways on that remote, and calls `get#SinglePaymentGatewayDetails` for each; the payload's status and amount are not applied. The demo data rows (`AuthorizeNetSandbox`, `SmatHmacSha512`, header `X-ANET-Signature`; `ShippoSandbox`, `SmatHmacSha256Timestamp`, header `Shippo-Auth-Signature`) carry no `sharedSecret`.
- **Browser card tokens.** `GET` and `POST /rest/s1/mantle/my/payments/{paymentId}/authorizeNet` and `.../payTrace` (`mr-mantle/service/mantle.rest.xml`) call `get#BrowserPaymentConfig` and `submit#BrowserPaymentToken` of `mantle.ext.account.gateway.AuthorizeNetServices` and `PayTraceServices`, which need a signed-in user. The caller must be the payment's payer (the user's party equals `Payment.fromPartyId`) or pass `mantle.account.PaymentServices.check#PaymentGatewayAccess` for the organization paid. The payment must be `PmntPromised` or `PmntDeclined` and of that gateway. The payment decides the organization, gateway, amount and currency; the browser gives the single-use token, the save consent and an operation id.

## Supplier

`mantle.order.SupplierPortalServices` `get#SupplierHome`, `get#QuoteInvitation`, `save#QuoteResponse`, `submit#QuoteResponse`, `get#PublishedPurchaseOrder`, `acknowledge#PurchaseOrder` and `report#DropShipDelivery` (`service/mantle/order/SupplierPortal.js`), and `mantle.account.PortalServices` `get#Submission` and `submit#SupplierBill` (`service/mantle/account/Portal.js`), each `authenticate="anonymous-all"`. Each finds the organization from the published site's `supplier` mount. The caller is a link's token or a signed-in Supplier contact of the order's supplier: a `Quote` token with `QuoteResponse` for a quote request, an `Order` token with `OrderAcknowledgement` for a purchase order, an `Order` token with `BillUpload` for a bill. A signed-in contact does not see a quote before its request was sent or a link was made for it. The order's link carries both order scopes, is bound to the order's revision and lasts 366 days; `QUOTE_LINK_DAYS` is 60. `revoke#OrderCapabilities` (`authenticate="false"`) revokes links on a status change; it reads the order's own status and does nothing when the status the caller names is not the order's. A link of an earlier revision than the order's is answered `SupersededRevision` and shows nothing of the newer one. The writes of `SupplierPortal.js` count against a per-requester limit of 60 posts in ten minutes (`supplier-post:`). A quote response takes prices only for lines of the order (an unknown line is refused) and a note cut to 1,000 characters. A delivery report's quantity is at most the line's open remainder. `submitSupplierBill` takes a bill number (1 to 60 characters), an optional date and amount and one file; the organization and supplier come from the checked capability; a repeat of the operation id or of the bill number answers the first submission; it counts against the Billing post limit (above); while five of the supplier's submissions for the purchase order are neither approved nor rejected (no invoice yet, or `InvoiceIncoming`/`InvoiceReceived`; `OPEN_BILL_LIMIT`) a new bill is refused (Conflict), and a declined or accepted one frees a place. `get#Submission`'s order shows no total for a link of an earlier revision or while a revision waits for the buyer's approval (SEC-PSS-24).

Files of Supplier, Support and Shop reviews go through `service/mantle/party/PortalMedia.js` `stage`. A file is at most 10,485,760 bytes. Its declared type must be `application/pdf`, `image/png` or `image/jpeg` and its leading bytes must be that type's signature; `sniff` reads the staged file's first eight bytes (SEC-PSS-65). With no `pass_scanner_service` or no `pass_media_root` property the upload is refused (`Unavailable`). The file is staged at `{pass_media_root}/{folder}/{32 random characters}` and passed to the scanner service; anything but `clean === true`, and a scanner error, deletes it and refuses the upload. The kept name is the upload's base name with every character outside `[a-zA-Z0-9_ -]` replaced, cut to 90 characters, plus the sniffed type's extension. Kept files go under `mantle.content.large.root` or `dbresource://mantle/content`.

## Support

### Help, cases and their files

`mantle.request.SupportCaseServices` and `mantle.request.KnowledgeServices` `get#CustomerArticle` and `rate#Article` (`service/mantle/request/SupportPortal.js`), each `authenticate="anonymous-all"`. Each finds the organization from the published site's `support` mount, which must be an internal organization with a service policy. An anonymous reader of the help gets the sources of the public audience (`SkaPublic`) only; a customer also the customer audiences. `rateArticle` is an anonymous write (`TOKEN_WRITES`), limited to 30 in ten minutes a requester and 500 an hour a site (SEC-PSS-25). Its `token` is the nonce `getCustomerArticle` answered: a random view id and the server's grant for the site, the article and that id, good for an hour (`ec.user.serverGrant("article-rating", ...)`, keyed by a key derived from the server's own secret); `rateArticle` takes only one `checkServerGrant` accepts (another article's, an edited or an unsigned one is Unprocessable). One rating is kept per digest of the nonce and the page, and a repeat answers the first.
