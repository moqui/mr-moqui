# Attack surface: PASS commerce and portals

The public applications that sell, bill and serve customers and suppliers: Shop, Billing, Supplier, Support, PASS Sales and PASS Marketing, as mr-pass pages and the Mantle services behind them. How every PASS page reaches the authority, public accounts and sites are in [Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS.md](Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS.md); Forum and Smithy are in [Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS-Forum-Smithy.md](Attack-Surface-Apps-PASS-Forum-Smithy.md). This is one file of the attack surface map. The index, how to read a row, the defaults and the lock-down advice are in [Attack-Surface.md](Attack-Surface.md).

## Shop

PASS Shop is `mantle.order.ShopCartServices`, `mantle.order.ShopCheckoutServices`, `mantle.party.ShopCustomerServices`, `mantle.product.ShopFulfillmentServices`, `mantle.product.ShopSubscriptionServices`, the review services of `service/mantle/product/StoreReviews.js` and the theme reads of `mantle.public.ThemeServices`, each service `authenticate="anonymous-all"`. The store comes from the site's enabled `shop` mount (`service/mantle/product/ShopContext.js` `shopOf`: a published site, a store of an internal organization), never from the request.

### Cart

`mantle.order.ShopCartServices` (PASS operations `getCart`, `addCartItem`, `updateCartItem`, `removeCartItem`, `applyPromoCode`, `removePromoCode`, `attachCart`, `releaseCart` and `recoverCart`; `service/mantle/order/ShopCart.js`). A cart is an open `OrderHeader` with a `StoreCartAccess` row. The caller is the holder of the cart capability (`input.token`, 48 random characters, only its digest stored, kept by the frontend in its server session) or the signed-in account, whose own cart is found by its party. No operation takes an order id. A capability reaches a cart only on the same site, store and currency, while the row is unrevoked and unexpired and the order open; a cart attached to an account is reached only by that account. Each capability lookup counts against the requester (600 in ten minutes). The first `addCartItem` with no cart makes one: an order and the access row, limited to 30 carts a requester in ten minutes and, for a guest's cart, the store's `PsstGuestCartsHourly` setting an hour (1,000 when unset; `cart-store:`, SEC-PSS-05). A guest's cart has no party until `beginCheckout` makes its guest `Party`, `Person` and `PartyRole` under the order's lock (the order part's customer and the access row's `guestPartyId`), so a cart that never reaches a checkout makes none. The request gives a product, an option, a quantity (1 to 999), a line id, a code, the revision seen and an operation id. The unit price is the authority's (`offersOf`) at each add and change. A cart holds 50 lines. A promo code is tried at most 20 times a requester in ten minutes and every refusal reads the same. A code is matched by its text, ignoring case, or by its `promoCodeId` (`storeCodeOf`), among the codes of the shop's store, whatever its `userEntered` flag ("Customers enter it"); Commerce's `store#PromoCode` and `generate#PromoCodes` create a code with no id, so its id is the entity's sequenced one, and the cart view lists an applied code by its text. `attachCart` needs a sign-in, attaches or merges the session's guest cart and rotates the capability. `releaseCart` rotates the capability of every open cart of the account on the site. `recoverCart` (`service/mantle/order/CartRecovery.js`) takes a one-time token (48 characters, its digest on the access row; mailed as `/Recover?recoveryToken=`) and needs the signed-in account whose cart it is; a visitor is answered 401 and the token is kept; lookups are limited to 120 a requester in ten minutes. The page `Recover?recoveryToken=` (`screen/Shop/ShopRecovery.js`) keeps any text of 1 to 128 characters in the session with no lookup and redirects before any theme renders; the session keeps a cart capability only from the authority's answer to `recoverCart`. A cart with a payment in progress or a submitted checkout is not changed, except by `attachCart`: it checks for a pending checkout only when it merges two carts, and attaching the session's guest cart to the account sets the order part's customer, runs `handle#OrderMajorChange` and reprices the lines at today's price (`update#OrderItem`) also while the checkout is `ScoPaymentPending` or `ScoPlacementPending`; the placement then refuses the changed order (`ORDER_CHANGED`), which leaves it for staff. The page's `Cart/state` (a same-origin `GET`, `screen/Shop/Cart.xml`) answers the session's form token as JSON (`formToken`) to the holder of the session cookie.

### Checkout and payment

`mantle.order.ShopCheckoutServices` (thirteen PASS operations, `beginCheckout` to `getOrderConfirmation`, all in `TOKEN_WRITES`; `service/mantle/order/ShopCheckout.js`). One `StoreCheckout` row coordinates a cart to one placement. Every write needs the cart's holder; a call reached any other way is NotFound. After submission the checkout is also read by its reference (the `checkoutId`) with a return key (40 random characters, made at each submit, only its digest kept, valid while the checkout is open and for a day after it completes), by the capability of a cart placed less than a day ago, or by the signed-in customer with the reference. The reference and key read a minimal view (the state, the amount due, the attempt's state and provider address) and the order's id, date and status; they are given no contact, address, line or order link. They also continue a pending payment: `getCheckoutPaymentForm` tests the attempt's status and amount, not how the checkout was reached, so the reference and key get a fresh hosted form (the result page's "Continue to the payment page" and `PaymentRelay`). No lookup by reference is counted (`checkoutOfReference`). The request gives contact fields, address fields or one of the account's own addresses, a delivery option of the current quote, a payment option, the revision and terms version seen, opt-ins and an operation id. No price, total, tax, delivery charge, customer or status is read from the request. `submitCheckout` re-quotes delivery, then under the order's lock checks every line's price and availability, the promotion limits, the quote, the tax and the terms; a change answers `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and nothing is charged. The payment's amount is the order's total. A total of zero or less skips the gateway. The gateway is called through `authorize#SinglePayment` with a return address on the site's canonical host and Shop mount (`/Checkout/return?ref=…&returnToken=…`), never a caller's origin. The payment's state is read from the `Payment` row's status and the latest authorization response (`service/mantle/account/PaymentAttempts.js`). The status is changed by the gateway lookup (`get#SinglePaymentGatewayDetails`), the provider's webhook and the reconciliation job, and, for an account checkout, by the account itself: the payment's `fromPartyId` is the account's party (`add#OrderPartPayment` from the order part), so the account is its payer for `POST /rest/s1/mantle/my/payments/{paymentId}/authorizeNet`, which authorizes it with a browser token while it is `PmntPromised` ([browser card tokens](#payment-callbacks-and-browser-card-tokens); how `authorize#SinglePayment` then moves the status was not traced). Nothing the browser's return brings back is read. A result read asks the provider at most once a second for each attempt. `place#Order` runs once the payment is approved, only when the order's revision and total are still the frozen ones; a provider amount that differs holds the order. A shopper's result read (`getCheckoutPaymentResult`) runs `refreshAttempt`, which places the order in its own step, and the order's approval starts `fulfill#ShopOrder`; nothing else of fulfillment is reached from a PASS route, and `fulfill#ShopOrder` and its steps are `authenticate` true. `getCheckoutPaymentForm` (fields `siteId`, `reference`, `key`) answers a fresh Authorize.Net Accept Hosted form address and token for a pending attempt whose amount equals the frozen amount, one page per reference; the token is not stored. Before it asks for the token, Authorize.Net's `hostedForm` searches the merchant's transactions for the reference (two unsettled list requests, the settled batch list of the last day and one list request per batch), and it then moves the pending answer's `hostedFormDate`; nothing paces or counts these calls. The provider's return address it makes carries the caller's `key`, which is checked only when the call came by the reference; by the cart, any text goes into it. A guest's order link (`orderToken`, the `SalesOrder` kind's `OrderView`, with `Download` for a digital order, 366 days) is issued once, only to a call that came by the cart's own capability. Stored operation results leave out the return key, the order token and the cart token.

### Relay and result pages of mr-pass

`screen/Shop/PaymentRelay.xml`, `MethodRelay.xml` and `PaymentResult.xml` are `require-authentication="false"` and run their script in `pre-actions`. `PaymentRelay` (`ShopCheckout.js` `relayPage`) reads `ref` (cut to 40 characters of `[A-Za-z0-9_-]`), calls `getCheckoutPaymentForm` with that reference and the key the session keeps for it, and writes a form whose `action` is the answered `formUrl` and whose hidden fields are the answered fields, each escaped; the page's same-origin script (`Checkout/enhance`) submits it. The checkout relay does not itself check the host of `formUrl`; the route's CSP `form-action` does. The amount and the form address are not the request's: the payment's amount must equal the frozen amount, and the form address is the gateway's constant. `Checkout/return` (`screen/Shop/Checkout.xml`, `method="get"`, `read-only="true"`, so neither the URL-parameter rule nor the cross-site rule for transitions applies) reads only `ref` and `returnToken` (or `key`), keeps the pair in the session's `passShopCheckout:{site}` map without asking whether it reads a checkout, asks the authority to look the payment up at the provider once and redirects to `PaymentResult?ref=`; the gateway's other parameters are ignored. Every later checkout call for that reference sends the kept key (`keyOf`, by reference alone), so a link from anyone puts their pair in the visitor's session. `PaymentResult` (`resultPage`) reads `ref`, calls `getCheckoutPaymentResult` with the session's key or cart, sends a completed checkout to `OrderConfirmation`, and otherwise writes the state with every value escaped and, for a pending hosted attempt, the provider's address or a link to `PaymentRelay`. `MethodRelay` (`ShopAccount.js` `relayPage`) reads the form the session kept from `beginPaymentMethodSetup`, removes it from the session, and writes it only when its `formUrl` starts `https://accept.authorize.net/` or `https://test.authorize.net/`; otherwise it redirects to `PaymentMethods`. Nothing reaches it from the card setup: `addMethod` redirects to `{mount}/PaymentMethods/relay`, which resolves to `/Shop/PaymentMethods/relay` and is Not Found (`PaymentMethods.xml` has no `relay` and no `allow-extra-path`); `MethodRelay` answers only at `{mount}/MethodRelay`, which no page links and whose CSP would not allow the provider either. `PaymentResult` has one transition, `state` (`method="get"`, `read-only="true"`), which answers the checkout's state for the reference and the session's key. The pages are `no-store` and cannot be framed (`frame-ancestors 'none'`, see the headers above). `PaymentMethods/add` and `PaymentMethods/end` are `method="post"`; `PaymentMethods/return` has no `method`: it runs actions and is reached by a GET with the URL parameter `setup`, which the framework refuses (400), and a provider's return to it is a cross-site GET (403), so neither an Authorize.Net nor a test gateway setup comes back through it.

### Orders and the guest order link

`mantle.party.ShopCustomerServices` (fourteen services; `service/mantle/party/ShopCustomer.js`). An order is reached by a guest's order link (`input.orderToken`, the `SalesOrder` `OrderView` capability, its one order; mailed and long-lived; it reads, and its cancellation (which applies at once under `customerImmediateCancel`), return and case in the customer's name each need a code `sendOrderChangeCode` mails to the order's address: eight digits made by the delivery's build (only a digest over the link and the code kept, on a `SalesOrder` capability of scope `OrderChange` bound to the link, never on a mailed link), lasting 15 minutes, used once, ended at its fifth wrong try, asked at most 5 times a requester in ten minutes and 5 an order an hour, SEC-PSS-23; which must be of the site's store; an `orderId` beside it must be its own) or by the signed-in account in the self relationship, for an order of the store whose every part has the account's party as customer and the store's organization as vendor. Another's order and an unknown one are the same NotFound. `findShopOrders`, `getShopOrder` and `getShopOrderDocument` are the link's reads; `getShopOrderDocument` answers only an invoice that is one of the reached order's. `previewReorder` and `reorder` drop the link and need the account. A verified account's first read merges into its party each guest party of a completed guest checkout of the store whose typed contact email equals the account's email (`mantle.party.DuplicateServices.merge#Parties`), when that party is still only the guest of that one cart.

### Addresses and payment methods

`get#Addresses`, `store#Address`, `end#Address`, `get#PaymentMethods`, `begin#PaymentMethodSetup`, `finish#PaymentMethodSetup` and `end#PaymentMethod` each need a signed-in account in the self relationship (`requireSelf`); a signed-in account outside it is `Unauthorized`. `store#Address` and `begin`/`finish#PaymentMethodSetup` first count against the requester, 30 in ten minutes (`shop-account:`, SEC-PSS-67). `store#Address` takes a name, two address lines, a city, a postal code, a country and a state (`address1` at most 255 characters, `city` 100, `postalCode` 60; the country must be a `moqui.basic.Geo` of type `GEOT_COUNTRY`, the state any existing `Geo`) and a purpose of billing or shipping. A change names a `contactMechId`, which must be a postal address with a current `PartyContactMech` of the caller's party, and the revision shown. An account keeps at most `limits.addresses` addresses. The store's carrier validation (`mantle.shipment.CarrierServices.validate#PostalAddress`) is called when the store has a shipping gateway with one; its suggestion is answered and not applied. `end#Address` ends only the caller's party's own purposes of that address. `begin#PaymentMethodSetup` calls the store gateway's hosted setup service with the caller's party, a 20-character reference and a return address made from the site's Shop mount (`/PaymentMethods/return?setup=…`), and answers the provider's redirect address or form. `finish#PaymentMethodSetup` calls the gateway's setup sync service with the caller's party and the request's `setupReference`. The setup gateway is the first store gateway enabled for the organization whose capabilities name both services (`StoreCheckout.js` `gatewayCapabilities`): Authorize.Net's `get#HostedProfileForm` and `sync#CustomerProfile`, the test gateway's `begin#HostedMethodSetup` and `finish#HostedMethodSetup`; PayTrace has none. Authorize.Net's form ensures the caller party's own customer profile and answers a hosted profile page token, never stored; its sync reads only that party's kept profile and keeps each new payment profile as a masked card of the party; neither reads `setupReference`, so what binds the sync is the caller's party, not the reference. The test gateway's `begin` answers the return address as its redirect, and its `finish` makes a test card ending 1111 for the party once per reference, a reference bound to no account and not single use. The page lets the request's `setup` win over the session's; with either gateway this reaches only the caller's own party. `end#PaymentMethod` ends only a method whose `ownerPartyId` is the caller's party and that is not ended; when recurring orders use it the call needs `confirm`, and each contract that renewed with it loses its consent. A card is entered on the gateway's hosted page; the list shows the brand, last four digits and expiry. `get#Addresses`, `end#Address`, `get#PaymentMethods` and `end#PaymentMethod` make no rate-limit call of their own.

### Subscriptions

`mantle.product.ShopSubscriptionServices` `get#Subscriptions`, `get#Subscription`, `begin#SubscriptionPurchase`, `change#SubscriptionMethod`, `cancel#Subscription` and `resume#Renewal` each need a signed-in account in the self relationship and a contract whose store is the site's and whose `customerPartyId` is the account's party; anything else is NotFound. A write names the contract's revision. A new card for renewals must be one of the account's own, at an off-session gateway. `resume#Renewal` and `change#SubscriptionMethod` charge the kept card at once: they reopen a renewal that failed for the current period, or one waiting for a retry, to a retry due now (`reopenStep`), and `advance` makes a new renewal payment and authorizes it as a merchant-initiated, stored-credential charge; neither counts against the requester, and the offer's retry days bound only the job. The renewal engine: `dispatch#Renewal` (the nightly hand-off) and `run#Renewals` (hourly) claim a due contract's period once under the contract's lock, clone the base with no payment, and charge only with recorded consent for the contract's own card, owned by its customer, as a stored-credential authorization with the original network transaction id; the result is applied under the occurrence's lock, and a decline retries at the offer's days with one notice each. A merge clears the consent. Cancellation closes waiting occurrences with no charge and refuses one in flight, and a cancelled contract is never charged. A refund revokes only the term the line paid and stops renewal when the latest term is refunded. A store's renewal gateways are its bound off-session gateways, without the organization's enabled rule that checkout applies. A store offers only its own organization's or shared products, so another organization's paid-access product cannot be bought.

### Digital downloads

`mantle.product.ShopFulfillmentServices` `get#Downloads`, `issue#Download` and `get#DownloadFile` (PASS operations `getDownloads`, `issueDownload`, `getShopDownload`). A grant (`StoreDownloadGrant`) is reached by an order link with the `Download` scope (its one order, with a completed checkout on this site) or by the signed-in verified account (its own grants in the store). `issueDownload` is limited to 60 a requester in ten minutes and makes a `SalesOrder` capability of scope `DownloadFile` for the grant's order that lasts ten minutes, which `getShopDownload` alone accepts: it is no order link and issues no successor (SEC-PSS-03). `getShopDownload` takes the grant id and that token, checks the capability against the grant's order and the store's organization, checks the grant again (unrevoked, in its dates, its fulfillment completed), counts the download and answers the file's location, which is the `contentLocation` of the grant's `ProductContent` row (type `PcntDownload`), and only when that location is under the content root in the product's own folder (`ShopFulfillment.js` `servedDownload` with `Content.js` `owned`: `dbresource://mantle/content` or Mantle's `mantle@content-root` or `mantle@content-large-root` setting, `product/<productId>/`, no `..`); any other answers NOT_AVAILABLE. `create#ProductContent` and `Content.js` `update` refuse such a location for a download row; the entity-auto `create#mantle.product.ProductContent` (mr-marble `createContentWithLocation`) still stores any location, which nothing serves as a download (SEC-PSS-01, fixed). The frontend streams it with authorization off, as an attachment with `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and `Cache-Control: no-store`, a 1 MiB window at a time, honouring one `bytes=start-end` range. The bundled frontend issues the token and streams in one request, so the token is not sent to the browser. The account page reaches that request by a link, `/Downloads/file?grant=…`: a GET with a URL parameter to a transition that runs actions, which the framework refuses (400), so the link does not download. Grants come from fulfillment (`fulfill#ShopOrder`, started by the order's approval): for an approved Shop order's digital and paid-access lines it captures, invoices, finalizes and settles, completes and grants or activates, and sends the notice, each step in its own transaction; a failed or declined capture holds the rows, and refunds and returns revoke grants and terms.

### Catalog

`mantle.product.ShopCatalogServices` (`service/mantle/product/ShopCatalog.js`). A product is shown only when the merchandising resolver answers it public in the store. `quoteSelection` prices a listed variant or the product at a quantity of 1 to 999; `priceOf` is `get#StoreProductPrice` for the store and the signed-in party, never cost; `stockOf` answers bands only. `getPage` takes a route of `[a-z0-9-/]` and answers the exact published page through `SitePages.js`; `getSitemap` lists the tree, up to 5,000 published candidates filtered by the resolver, and the live pages. A product's name and descriptions (`contentTexts`: the store's `PcntProductName`, `PcntDescriptionLong` or `PcntDescription` row) are read from the row's `contentLocation` through the resource facade (`ec.resource.getLocationText`), whatever its scheme (`file:`, `runtime://`, `component://`, `classpath://`, `http`, `https`), or from its `description` when it has none, and published to every visitor: the product cards, the product page (`getProduct`), `getShopPage`, the sitemap, the cart's offers and the store feeds (`StoreFeeds.js`), the description sanitized as rich text, which keeps the text. No location rule applies to these rows, as it does to images and downloads: `create#ProductContent` keeps a given `contentLocation` for any type but `PcntDownload`, as do `Content.js` `update` and mr-marble's entity-auto `createContentWithLocation`; `Content.js` `save` refuses a foreign location. The mr-pass catalog pages (`screen/Shop/Catalog.js` `originOf`) put the request's own scheme, host and port into the hreflang link, the product's JSON-LD, `robots.txt`'s `Sitemap:` line and every `<loc>` of `sitemap.xml`, answers that may be `public, max-age=60`.

### Product images and reviews

`getShopProductImage` answers a `ProductContent` image of a product public in the store now, only when its `contentLocation` starts `dbresource://` or `component://`, has no `..`, backslash or encoded dot or slash, and ends in png, jpg, jpeg, gif or webp; the content type is the extension's. It is `public, max-age=60` only when the request names the site's current generation. `getShopReviewImage` answers a review image by a digest (16 to 128 characters) only while its review is approved, of the site's store, and its product public; the content type is the stored `image/png` or `image/jpeg`. `submitProductReview` counts against the requester first (30 in ten minutes), then needs a signed-in account with a verified email and, for a first review, a delivered order line of the product; an edit must name the caller's own review. The rating is 1 to 5 and the body at most 5,000 characters of plain text. A photo is staged only after those checks. It must be declared `image/png` or `image/jpeg`, at most three a review, and goes through `service/mantle/party/PortalMedia.js` `stage` (the rules under [Supplier](#supplier)): at most 10,485,760 bytes, the leading bytes must be the declared type's, and the scanner must answer clean. It is kept under the content root at `productReview/{store}/{review}/{seq}.{png|jpg}`, a path made from ids. A new or edited review is `PrvwPending` until staff approve it.

### Themes

A store's theme is a package a Commerce user writes (`COMMERCE_THEME_EDIT`) and publishes (`COMMERCE_THEME_PUBLISH`) in the stores of the user's scope. It is rendered on the public Shop pages of the site (the page kinds home, not-found, category, product, search, page, cart and checkout).

- **The package.** `service/mantle/public/Themes.js` takes paths that match `^[A-Za-z0-9_-][A-Za-z0-9_.-]*(/[A-Za-z0-9_-][A-Za-z0-9_.-]*)*$`, text files ending `.json`, `.html.fancy-locked`, `.css` or `.svg`, and binary files ending `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`, `.webp` or `.woff2`: at most 300 files, 512 KiB a file and 8 MiB in all. The renderer's own bounds (`crates/moqui-core/src/script/theme/package.rs`) are 2,000 files, 1 MiB a text file, 1 MiB an assembled template and 16 MiB a binary asset named by reference. The renderer takes the package as text; a binary asset is only a reference (`{digest, size, contentType}`) and its bytes are not part of a render. The layout is the whole HTML document (the starter's `layouts/store.html.fancy-locked` begins `<html>` and `<head>`).
- **Expressions.** Each `${}` expression is parsed and checked before any runs (`expr.rs`). A denied name is refused as a variable or a property: `ec`, `sri`, `context`, `raw`, `globalThis`, `eval`, `Function`, `AsyncFunction`, `GeneratorFunction`, `constructor`, `__proto__`, `prototype`, the `__define*__` and `__lookup*__` names, `defineProperty`, `defineProperties`, `setPrototypeOf`, `getPrototypeOf`, `Reflect`, `Proxy`, `Symbol`, `arguments`, `require`, `import`, `fetch`, `XMLHttpRequest`, `WebSocket`, `setTimeout`, `setInterval`, `queueMicrotask`, `Atomics`, `SharedArrayBuffer`, `WebAssembly`, and any name that starts with `$`. A computed property is checked only when it is a string literal or a template literal with no expression; a property name built at run time (`x["con" + "structor"]`) is not seen by this check. `this`, `new`, a function, a class, an assignment, `++` and `--`, `import`, `yield`, `await`, a tagged template, `super`, `delete` and an arrow function with a block body are refused. Each layout, section and block runs through `template::render_locked_bounded` (`compose.rs`), with the view as a JSON snapshot whose values are the template's names, a global object frozen with everything reachable from it (`crates/moqui-core/src/script/template.rs` `SANDBOX_LOCKDOWN`), and that renderer's own ceilings of 5 seconds and 32 MiB. A template's other names are the engine's (`format`, `localize`, `t`, the frozen built-ins), and code made at run time runs in the same frozen sandbox. The Mantle side renders only to validate (`Themes.js`), with the page kind, a sample view that holds no store record, placeholder components, `assetBase`, `releaseId` and a page time of two seconds; the live and preview renders (`ShopTheme.js`, `ShopPreview.js`) pass the contract view, the components, `references`, `media`, `sources` (none, or the release's), `assetBase` and `releaseId`, and nothing else.
- **Markup.** Template source is checked before it runs and each render's output is checked again (`markup.rs`, modes `Source` and `Output`). Refused elements: `script`, `iframe`, `frame`, `frameset`, `object`, `embed`, `applet`, `form`, `base`, `portal`, `noscript`, `xmp`, `plaintext`, `noembed`, `noframes`, `math`, `isindex`, `keygen`, `fencedframe`, and the SVG elements `foreignobject`, `animate`, `animatemotion`, `animatetransform`, `set`, `discard`, `handler`, `listener`, and a `style` inside an SVG. Refused attributes: any whose name starts with `on`, and `srcdoc`, `formaction`, `formmethod`, `formenctype`, `formtarget`, `formnovalidate`, `form`, `action`, `ping`, `http-equiv` and `is`. A `<link>` must have a `rel` of `stylesheet`, `icon`, `apple-touch-icon`, `preload`, `canonical`, `alternate`, `next`, `prev`, `author` or `license`. A tag inside `<title>` or `<textarea>`, an unclosed `<style>`, `<title>` or `<textarea>`, an `<svg>` left open in a render's output, and markup that ends inside a tag are refused. A `<meta>` must be a `charset`, a `name` of `viewport`, `description`, `keywords`, `author`, `robots`, `googlebot`, `theme-color`, `color-scheme`, `application-name`, `generator` or `format-detection` or a `twitter:` one, an Open Graph `property` or an `itemprop` (`meta_allowed`). A tag that has a `charset` or an `itemprop` attribute is allowed whatever its `name`, so `name="referrer"` is refused alone (SEC-PSS-62) but not beside either of them. The `referrerpolicy` attribute is not on the deny list, so a theme's link may carry its own referrer policy. The deny lists do not hold `link`, `style`, `input`, `button`, `select`, `img`, `video` or `audio`.
- **URLs and CSS.** The `href` of `a` and `area` (and of a `link` with a navigation `rel`) may be a relative address or an `http`, `https`, `mailto` or `tel` address of any host. A resource address (`src`, `poster`, `data`, `background`, `cite`, `manifest`, `srcset`, a stylesheet's `href`) may be relative, on an approved origin, or a `data:` PNG, GIF, JPEG, WebP or AVIF image; any other scheme is refused. A `style` attribute, a `<style>` element and SVG paint attributes that hold `url` are checked as CSS, which refuses `@import`, `expression(`, `javascript:`, `vbscript:`, `-moz-binding`, `</style`, `<!--`, `-->`, `image-set(`, `src(` and the `behavior` properties. The approved origins are the render request's `allowedOrigins`; no caller passes any (`Themes.js` and `ThemeAuthoring.js` validation, mr-pass's `screen/Shop/ShopTheme.js` `renderView` and `ShopPreview.js`), so a theme's resource address is relative to the site or a `data:` image.
- **Limits.** A page is bounded to 100 sections, 100 blocks, composition depth 8, include depth 8, 2 MiB of output, one second a render (a layout, a section or a block), and the locked renderer's page time and memory. The `theme-limits` element of the conf can lower each value, never raise it.
- **What a theme sees.** `get#ShopPage` answers the theme an allow-listed view (`service/mantle/public/ThemeView.js` `contract()`: the fields its `shapes` name, dropping any name that is or ends in secret, token, password, partyid or userid). The authority's cart and checkout views carry the store's data only. About the visitor, a product page's view carries the visitor's own review state, and the frontend adds after `contract()` the shopper's cart summary on the cart page (`view.cart`, `{itemCount, subtotal}`) and `view.page.title` on every page it renders through the checkout kind, which on the account, order, invoice, review and subscription pages names the shopper's record (the order id, the invoice id, the reviewed product, the subscription's name). About other sites a view says nothing; every read is through the request's site. - **Reads and assets.** `mantle.public.ThemeServices` `get#ShopThemeRelease`, `get#ShopThemeFiles` and `get#ShopThemeAsset` (`CLIENT_READS`) answer only the release that is the site's Shop mount's active one (`RELEASE_NOT_ACTIVE`, NotFound, for another id). `getShopThemeFiles` takes 1 to 50 paths and answers only files the release pins. `getShopThemeAsset` answers only a binary path the release pins, named with the digest it pins; the bytes at the pinned version are checked against that digest before the location is answered; the type is the extension's (`image/png`, `image/jpeg`, `image/gif`, `image/webp`, `font/woff2`). The BOSS Commerce Website and page screens' 41 `service-call`s each pass an explicit `in-map`, and their script transitions pass named values.
