# Attack surface: public applications (mr-pass and Mantle)

The public applications: mr-pass and the Mantle (mr-mantle) services its operations reach. The PASS authority's gRPC service is a row under [Host routes and servlets](Attack-Surface-Routes.md#host-routes-and-servlets), and its edge link form is under [Edge node](Attack-Surface-Cluster-Edge.md#edge-node). The staff applications are in [Attack-Surface-Apps-BOSS.md](Attack-Surface-Apps-BOSS.md). This is one file of the attack surface map. The index, how to read a row, the defaults and the lock-down advice are in [Attack-Surface.md](Attack-Surface.md).

## PASS Sales

PASS Sales' customer quotes (`mantle.order.SalesPortalServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`, `authenticate="anonymous-all"`, reached only as the PASS operations `findSalesQuotes`, `getSalesQuote`, `getSalesQuoteDocument`, `respondSalesQuote` and `getSalesQuoteResponse` of the authority's allowlist): each finds the organization from the site's `sales` mount and the customer from the bearer link's capability (the `SalesOrder` kind's QuoteView and QuoteRespond scopes, bound to one issue's commercial revision, ending with the quote's validity, made only by `send#Quote` for each recipient's mail, revoked by `revise#Quote` and `withdraw#Quote`; `create#AccessCapability` refuses those scopes) or from a signed-in account's contact grant of the `sales` app or its own verified customer party, never from the request; a link reaches its one issue while it stands and its receipt after a response, never a list; a signed-in account sees a quote of a customer it acts for only at its issued revision or once answered; a wrong, expired, revoked, crossed or ended link answers the same NotFound; every token lookup and every response counts against the requester's limit; `respondSalesQuote` is the only bearer write (`TOKEN_WRITES`), runs respond#Quote's lifecycle under the order's lock (the revision seen, the terms acknowledged, the acceptor named and the threshold checked there; one response an issue, `place#Order` once), and its operation key binds the capability or the customer, the organization, the issue and the payload; above the seller's acceptance threshold a link alone cannot accept; answers are allow-listed projections and the issued PDF is streamed by the facade, its location never answered; link pages are no-store with a same-origin referrer policy. A link's token is in the sent mail the organization keeps. Invite to Sales (`mantle.sales.AccountServices.invite#SalesContact`) needs `SALES_CUSTOMER_INVITE`, a customer account of the caller's sales scope, one of the contact's own addresses and an organization of the scope with a published Sales site.

PASS Sales' customer orders (`mantle.order.SalesPortalServices` find#CustomerOrders, get#CustomerOrder and get#OrderDocument, all `allow-remote="false"`, `authenticate="anonymous-all"`, reached only as the PASS operations `findSalesOrders`, `getSalesOrder` and `getSalesOrderDocument` of the authority's allowlist, reads with no bearer write): each finds the organization from the site's `sales` mount and the customer from the bearer link's capability (the `SalesOrder` kind's OrderView, made for each recipient of the storeless confirmation at each delivery attempt, lasting a year, only its digest kept, not ended by the order's cancellation for a storeless order's OrderView link (it opens the order marked Cancelled, read only; Download and a store order's link still end); or issued by `create#AccessCapability` under the kind's permission as before) or from a signed-in account's contact grant of the `sales` app or its own verified customer party, never from the request; a link reaches its one order and never lists, and an order ID beside it must be its own; only a placed storeless sales order of that organization whose every part is the seller's to that customer, not a recurring base or template and not rejected, is shown; a cancelled one is shown read only, marked Cancelled, with Support's ask page alone and no cancellation, return or payment link, and never counted open; a store order's OrderView link opens Shop's page and the Sales page refuses it, and a quote link never opens an order nor an order link a quote; a wrong, expired, revoked or crossed link and another customer's order answer the same NotFound; every token lookup counts against the requester's limit; answers are allow-listed projections (no cost, margin, internal note or other party; a carrier's tracking link only when https); Billing is linked only for a signed-in account and Support only where the site mounts each for the same organization, each checking its own access; a link's reader reaches Support's requests for that one order through the link the page keeps in its session after a successful read, never in a URL; the order's PDF, the stored confirmation as placed, is streamed by the facade, its location never answered. A link's token is in the sent confirmation the organization keeps.

## PASS Marketing

PASS Marketing's public subscription (`mantle.marketing.MarketingPortalServices`, all `allow-remote="false"`, `authenticate="anonymous-all"`, reached only as the PASS operations `getMarketingPublication`, `requestSubscription`, `getSubscriptionRequest` and `verifySubscription` of the authority's allowlist): each finds the organization from the site's `marketing` mount (a published site, an internal organization) and a list only by its explicit public offer on that site (the policy's offer, site and open interval; the legacy `isPublic` flag offers nothing), never from the request; a site without the mount, an unoffered, closed, archived, mailbox or another organization's list and a forged, expired or another site's link answer the same NotFound. `requestSubscription` is an anonymous write (the transport lets it through by name; the authority takes it from a registered deployment as a TOKEN_WRITES operation) bounded by PASS's request key (a repeat answers the first request), at most ten topics, and limits per client (30 in ten minutes), site (1,000 an hour) and address (5 an hour), over which it answers the same receipt and keeps nothing; it always answers the same receipt (a known or new address, a member already, a refusal), echoes no address and never says whether a party was matched or made (never a personal contact). Its verification message is a transactional delivery of Comms' ledger from a ready transactional sending profile of the organization (a promotional one never sends it: the request is kept and the readiness says so), attempted on a service worker once the request commits, so the answer takes the same time whether or not a message goes out with the list policy's published confirmation template, at most 3 a day to an address; it carries the template's own wording, the topics' names and a link, never the visitor's text. Each message's link is a 40-character random token whose digest alone is kept, ending with the request (seven days); every lookup counts against the requester's limit (100 in ten minutes). `getSubscriptionRequest` is read only (a scanner's GET changes nothing) and answers the address masked; `verifySubscription` (a TOKEN_WRITES bearer write, a POST with the session token locally) accepts exactly the request's topics for its address under the request's lock, once (a replay answers the first result), with the request's own evidence date, so a withdrawal after the request is never undone by its link. The Account Email preferences page's Marketing context changes only the token's or the signed-in account's own party: a topic turned on is a request with its verification message (a signed-in verified address accepted at once), `unsubscribe#ContactListParty` with `organizationPartyId` withdraws for the token list's organization only. Shop's store-news choice is filed through the same evidenced path (source PcsCheckout), pending until the receipt's link or a verified sign-in.

PASS Marketing's forms (`mantle.marketing.MarketingPortalServices` get#Form and submit#Form, `allow-remote="false"`, `authenticate="anonymous-all"`, reached only as the PASS operations `getMarketingForm` and `submitMarketingForm` of the authority's allowlist) find the organization from the site's `marketing` mount and a publication only of a Marketing purpose (survey, lead form, registration questions) of that organization, on that site and open now: a public or signed-in accounts' one by its id, an invited one only by its link's token (an ExternalAccessCapability of the FormInvitation kind, scope `MarketingSurvey`, a 48-character token whose digest alone is kept, made at each attempt of an invitation send's delivery and ending with the publication or after 30 days; each lookup counts against the requester's limit, 100 in ten minutes); a site without the mount, a draft, closed or another site's publication and a forged, expired or revoked token answer the same NotFound, the link check's refusal answered in the page's own words. The form is the publication's as of now on the server (the customer-visible fields, never an internal field, a draft or a score); an answer goes through Commons' `mantle.party.FormPublicationServices.accept#Submission`, which refuses an unknown or internal field and a value for a field its rules hide (the visitor is told to leave such questions unanswered, naming none), checks each value's type and length, keeps one FormSubmission per request key (the same answers answer the first, others are refused), under the publication's or the invitation's lock, so a concurrent repeat answers the first, answers an invitation once under its lock and an account's publication once per party, and keeps the response as submitted (the framework's update and delete of it are refused). `submitMarketingForm` is an anonymous write (the transport lets it through by name; the authority takes it from a registered deployment as a TOKEN_WRITES operation) bounded by PASS's request key and limits per client (30 in ten minutes) and site (1,000 an hour); over either, an answer over 64 KB, a value over 4,000 characters, an invitation already answered, or one naming a field the page never offered gets the publication's same receipt and nothing is kept. An email address typed beside an answer never identifies or subscribes anyone: the subscription choice is a separate public request of the publication's list, filed after the answer through the subscription path (pending, its own verification message and limits). Staff read responses only through `mantle.marketing.MarketingFormServices` (a reader of the purpose and organization, only submissions to a Marketing publication, so no case feedback; the respondent named for an audience reader only). An invitation goes only to a recipient an invitation send's preparation and dispatch admit (the topic's membership, consent within the organization, suppression), approved and released as every send.

PASS Marketing's landing pages (`mantle.marketing.MarketingPortalServices.get#Page`, `allow-remote="false"`, `authenticate="anonymous-all"`, read only, reached only as the PASS operation `getMarketingPage` of the authority's allowlist, in no TOKEN_WRITES list) find the organization from the site's `marketing` mount (a published site, an internal organization) and a page only of that mount by its route, through the site page services' `get#SitePage`: the binding's exact published wiki version in its window; a site without the mount and a draft, scheduled, withdrawn or unknown page answer the same NotFound (the page a 404). The page's Markdown is made clean HTML by the authority (`cleanHtml`) and sanitized again on the page by the theme's rich text rule (ammonia's allow-list: no script, event or style attribute, SVG, `javascript:` or `data:` URL), under PASS's default no-store headers and the shell's CSP and a same-origin referrer policy; no stored HTML or expression runs. A component is embedded only by a paragraph naming an id (`[[subscribe ID]]`, `[[form ID]]`, `[[event ID]]`, at most five in all), which the page renders through `getMarketingPublication`, `getMarketingForm` or `getMarketingEvent`, so only a list offered, a form open or an event offer published on that site shows and a draft or another organization's record shows nothing; a subscribe box posts Subscribe's own request with its limits and neutral receipt. Staff write landing pages through `mantle.public.ThemeServices` and `mantle.public.SiteServices.publish#SitePage`, which take a page's app from its mount: a Marketing mount's page needs MARKETING_CONTENT_EDIT in the mount's organization (Marketing's scope), a Shop's its store and `COMMERCE_*`, so neither app's users reach the other's pages; the text is a page of the organization's page space (restricted updates, written by those services alone as the system after their checks). Blog posts are written through `mantle.marketing.MarketingContentServices`, which checks MARKETING_CONTENT_EDIT and the wiki's own write right to the space and the post's page before `create#WikiBlog`, `update#WikiBlog` and a category's entity write; a campaign's link to content publishes nothing, and a newsletter send takes only posts with a published page version and a reached publish date, in a space and on a page with no view restriction, escaped into the locked body.

PASS Marketing's tracking redirect and pixel are web scripts of mr-pass (`script/TrackingRedirect.js` at `/trk/*`, `script/TrackingPixel.js` at `/trk-o/*`, registered by mr-pass's own `MoquiConf.xml` on the authority and on a remote frontend alike, each with a per-client `<rate-limit>`, 60 and 120 a minute, when per-client web protection is on). Each declines the session (`ec.web.declineSession()`: no session cookie, token or Visit), runs as the anonymous request, and reaches the authority only through PASS's facade and transport (in process, REST or gRPC) by two operations of the allowlist whose only fields are the site, the code or token, the visitor key and the kind: `resolveTrackingLink` (`mantle.marketing.TrackingServices.resolve#TrackingLink`, read only, in no TOKEN_WRITES list) and `recordTrackingEvent` (`record#TrackingEvent`, an anonymous write the transport lets through by name and the authority takes from a registered deployment as a TOKEN_WRITES operation), both `allow-remote="false"`, `authenticate="anonymous-all"`. A link is `/trk/{siteId}/{code}`: the code is random and the authority answers it only for a published site that is the link's own (another site's, an unknown or a malformed code is the same NotFound, a neutral 404 page that echoes nothing). The destination comes only from the link: its registered destination, a landing page of its organization's Marketing mount or an https address on a host of a site that serves the organization or on the organization's allowlist (both checked when staff save the link through `store#TrackingLink`, MARKETING_CAMPAIGN_EDIT in the campaign's organization; no user name, no other scheme), with its own UTM values; a disabled or ended link goes to its safe destination, checked the same way when saved, or to the neutral page, and records nothing and sets no key; with no safe destination its answer is the unknown code's. No query parameter but `token` is read, so a destination, fallback or return address in the request is never followed, and the facade refuses an operation input naming one. The redirect sets nothing but the visitor key, a random first-party `mkt_vk` cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure over https, a year) set by a `Set-Cookie` header only when the organization's policy counts unique visitors (off by default) or the link is a consenting recipient's, never for a browser sending Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track or for a known automated fetch; the authority keeps only its digest and never a party id. Responses are no-store with no referrer. Recording is limited by the authority per client and code (30 in ten minutes; a registered frontend's forwarded visitor address) and per site (20,000 an hour); over a limit, and whenever recording fails, the visitor is still redirected (or given the image) and nothing is kept. A known scanner, crawler, prefetch or HEAD is recorded apart as automated and is never a conversion. A send's message carries personalized links and the pixel only for a recipient who allows tracking (PcpTracking) under a sending profile that tracks consenting recipients: each attempt's 40-character token rides in a `token` query parameter (redacted from the request log), its digest alone kept for ninety days; the recipient is named on an event only while they still allow tracking, checked when it comes; an expired or forged token names no one. Every other recipient gets each link's direct destination and no pixel. A message's link is personalized only on a live host of its own site. A landing page's address with a link's code (`?tc=`) records a page visit with the key the browser already holds, through the same operation; the page sets no cookie. Staff read links and their aggregate observations through `mantle.marketing.TrackingServices` (any Marketing reader of the organization; the observations for an author or a report reader; no person), and Commerce reads its stores' visit counts through `count#StoreVisits` after its own store check.

PASS Marketing's Event page (`mantle.marketing.MarketingPortalServices.get#Event`, `allow-remote="false"`, `authenticate="anonymous-all"`, read only, reached only as the PASS operation `getMarketingEvent` of the authority's allowlist, in no TOKEN_WRITES list, rendered at `/marketing/Event?offer={id}`) finds the organization from the site's `marketing` mount (a published site, an internal organization) and answers an event registration offer (EventRegistrationPolicy) only when it is published, of that organization, on that site, for an occurrence of an event not cancelled that has not ended; a site without the mount, a draft, closed, past or another site's or organization's offer and an unknown or malformed id answer the same NotFound (a 404). The answer is allow-listed: the offer's public title and description (never the event's own name or notes), the occurrence's dates in the offer's zone, its place or that it is online (never the call link, which goes to registrants only), each ticket's name, its price text from the ticket product's own prices (no amount is stored on the offer) and advisory availability, whether registration is open and the terms; no attendee, organizer, room, budget, campaign, work effort or party id. The page escapes every value and shows the description and terms as text, under PASS's default no-store headers, the shell's CSP and a same-origin referrer policy; a landing page shows an offer only through the same operation (`[[event ID]]`). Staff set registration up through `mantle.work.EventRegistrationServices`, which asks MARKETING_EVENT_MANAGE in the event's organization (kept by RegistrationEvent, made only by an events manager who may edit the event under the calendar's own rules) for every write and a Marketing read for every read, another organization's event or offer answering as a missing one, each write at the revision read; an event's campaign link is the campaign's own write (`CampaignServices.store#CampaignAssociation`, MARKETING_CAMPAIGN_EDIT); an offer's registration questions are a form publication of the organization that SubmitForm never serves on its own (answered only with a registration); publishing asks a published site of the organization's Marketing mount, a ready transactional sending profile and, for a priced ticket, a payment gateway of the organization or its store.

PASS Marketing's registration (`mantle.marketing.MarketingPortalServices` `reserve#Registration`, `complete#Registration`, `start#RegistrationPayment`, `get#Registration` and `cancel#Registration`, each `allow-remote="false"`, `authenticate="anonymous-all"`, reached only as the PASS operations `reserveRegistration`, `completeRegistration`, `startRegistrationPayment`, `getRegistration` and `cancelRegistration` of the authority's allowlist, all POSTs in the three TOKEN_WRITES lists, rendered at `/marketing/Register` and `/marketing/Registration`) finds the organization from the site's `marketing` mount and reserves only on an offer published on that site, open now; a reservation is an anonymous write bounded by PASS's request key (a repeat answers the first; the same key with other values is refused) and the authority's limits (ten a client in ten minutes, 500 a site an hour, five a buyer's address an hour), past which it is refused alike and holds nothing; the buyer is a party made or matched by the address without saying which. Its places are held or waitlisted together under the offer's and its tickets' row locks, so the seats are never oversold. Every later operation is reached by the reservation's capability (ExternalAccess `EventRegistration`, scopes RegistrationView and RegistrationCancel, its token answered once to the reserving request or put in a receipt or the buyer's ticket (never another attendee's), stored as a digest only and kept in the visitor's session; a receipt's or the buyer's ticket's link carries it once (`?token=`), read into the session and the address replaced), by the buyer's own verified account, or for the payment provider's return by the reservation's reference and a return key (its digest only), which reads the state alone; an unknown, expired or another site's capability and a bad key answer the same NotFound, and every token lookup counts against the requester's limit first. The payment is Accounting's typed attempt (PapEventRegistration) with the gateway's operation key, its amount the native order's total (the ticket products' own prices, X1's or the store's tax), never a value from the request; the payment step shows the tickets' prices and Pay sends that amount's key, a changed price refused (PRICE_CHANGED) and nothing charged; the browser's return confirms nothing: the verified result is applied under the offer's lock, and a success after the hold ended takes no seat. The answer names each place's attendee, ticket and state, the payment and refund state and the event's public facts; never another attendee's answers, an entry code's digest, the order's lines or a party id. An entry code is shown only in a confirmed place's ticket or by Show my entry code (a POST with a fresh request key); it is derived from the registration and its credential revision with the offer's secret (an encrypted field, never shown or listed), stored only as a digest with the revision, the same for a revision; a correction, a substitution or a staff reissue (MARKETING_EVENT_MANAGE, with a reason) raises the revision and a cancellation clears it, each revoking the earlier code; the link to join an online event goes to a confirmed place only. A cancellation needs the RegistrationCancel scope, a confirmation and a request key, before the occurrence starts and as the cancellation rule of the terms the registration accepted allows (a confirmed place until its deadline, after it with no refund only where the rule allows; else CANCELLATION_CLOSED and nothing changes); a confirmed place's refund is the rule's percentage of its price and tax, a place not yet confirmed is refunded in full, never a value from the request. Staff registrations go through `mantle.work.EventRegistrationServices`, which asks MARKETING_EVENT_MANAGE in the offer's organization for every read and write (another organization's registration answering as a missing one), each write at the revision read; the registration's files and Chatter are its events managers' only. A provider page that takes a posted form (Accept Hosted) gets a fresh token from `getRegistrationPaymentForm` (`get#RegistrationPaymentForm`, in the TOKEN_WRITES lists) by the capability or the buyer's account only, while the places are held, posted from `/marketing/RegistrationRelay`, the one Marketing route whose CSP allows Authorize.Net's form-action; the return key is made anew for its return address and reads the state alone.
